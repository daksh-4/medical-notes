# Bundle Branch Block

When both bundle branches blocked leads to AV block [[AV Blocks]]

One blocked
- Does not cause AV block 
- Normal PR interval 
- QRS will be prolonged
	- The working branch will begin depolarisation but takes longer to depolarise all the myocytes on the other side leading to a longer QRS complex
	- The current moving from one side to the other results in varying QRS complexes

Look at V1 and which direction the QRS is pointing - up in right

May progress to AV block - need for a pacemaker

Interferes with detection of ischaemia due to ST elevations and T wave inversion

Often caused by slowly progressive fibrosis/sclerosis
- More common in older patients
- Can result from structural heart disease
	- LBBB - prior MI, cardiomyopathy
	- RBBB - right heart failure

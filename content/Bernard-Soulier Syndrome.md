# Bernard-Soulier Syndrome

Qualitative disorder 

Genetic GP1b deficiency; platelet adhesion is impaired because they can't bind to vWf.

Blood smear shows mild thrombocytopenia with enlarged platelets. If GP1b is not functioning well, the platelets don't last as long and die, they also are immature platelets which is why they are large.

# Reye Syndrome

A. Fulminant liver failure and encephalopathy in children with viral illness who take

aspirin

1. Likely related to mitochondrial damage of hepatocytes  
B. Presents with hypoglycemia, elevated liver enzymes, and nausea with vomiting; may progress to coma and death

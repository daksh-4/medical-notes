# Adenocarcinoma of the Bladder

1. Malignant proliferation of glands, usually involving bladder
    
2. Arises from a urachal remnant (tumor develops at the dome of the bladder), cystitis glandularis, or exstrophy (congenital failure to form the caudal portion of the anterior abdominal and bladder walls)

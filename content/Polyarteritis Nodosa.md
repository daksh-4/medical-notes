# Polyarteritis Nodosa

Medium vessel

Necrotising [[Vasculitis]]
Involving most organs EXCEPT THE LUNG

Presents in young adults depending on the vessel involved
- Hypertension (renal artery)
- Abdominal pain with melena (mesenteric artery)
- Neurologic disturbances
- Skin lesions
- Associated with serum HBsAg

Lesions of varying stage are present 
- 'String of pearls' appearance on imaging
	- Early lesions have transmural inflammation of the vessel with characteristic fibrinoid necrosis
	- When it heals, you get fibrosis leading to a bumpy area on the vessel. 
	- Weakening of the blood vessel wall can results in aneurysm.
	- You have regions of aneurysms that are interspaced which is the string of pearls.

Pink represents fibrinoid necrosis

Treat with steroids or cyclophosphamide, fatal if not treated

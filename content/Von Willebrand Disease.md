# Von Willebrand Disease

Genetic vWf deficiency 
Most common inherited coagulation disorder

Multiple subtypes exist - cause quantitative or qualitative deficiencies. Most common type is autosomal dominant with decreased vWf levels. 

Low vWf impairs platelet adhesion - leading to primary haemostasis symptoms such as mild mucosal and skin bleeding. 

Lab findings 

Increased bleeding time
Increased PTT (vWf helps in stabilising factor 8, so loss increases the PTT) normal PT
Abnormal ristocetin test - normally ristocetin would cause all the platelets in a sample to aggregate however this wouldn't happen in vWf disease

Treatment 

Desmopressin - increases vWf release from Weibel-Palade bodies of the endothelial cells.

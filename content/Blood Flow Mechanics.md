# Blood Flow Mechanics

Velocity * Area = Flow

Compliance = volume/pressure

If you rearrange pressure = volume/compliance so the pressure must increase to move a certain volume through a low compliance vessel. 

Pressure = cardiac output x total peripheral resistance

![[Pasted image 20241102115719.png|500]]

Mean arterial pressure = diastolic plus 1/3 (Systolic - Diastolic)

Flow = Velocity x Area

Eccentric hypertrophy 
- Longer myocytes because more sarcomeres added in series
- ![[Pasted image 20241102120940.png|500]]
- Left ventricular mass increased 
- Wall thickness is not increased
- Classically occurs in volume overload so AR or MR and cardiomyopathy

Concentric hypertrophy
- Pressure overload chronically
- Sarcomeres added in parallel 
- Left ventricular mass increased 
- Wall thickness increased
- ![[Pasted image 20241102121109.png|500]]
- Hypertension and aortic stenosis 
- Both raise pressure in LV cavity 
- Decreased compliance due to thickness
- Often seen in diastolic heart failure

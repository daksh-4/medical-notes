# Varcicocele

1. Dilation of the spermatic vein due to impaired drainage
    
2. Presents as scrotal swelling with a "bag of worms" appearance
    
3. Usually left sided; left testicular vein drains into the left renal vein, while the right
    
    testicular vein drains directly into the IVC.

l. Associated with left-sided renal cell carcinoma; RCC often invades the renal vein. D. Seen in a large percentage of infertile males

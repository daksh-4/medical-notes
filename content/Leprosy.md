[[Infectious Disease]]

Tuberculoid leprosy is the least severe form and is often self-limited. Mycobacterial infection is limited by an intact CMI response, Th1-mediated. Mouth skin papules develop and are associated with hypopigmentation, hair follicle loss, and focally decreased sensation. 

Lepromatous leprosy is the most severe form. It occurs in patients with a weak CMI response. Macrophage signalling to kill M. leprae is limited, leading to mycobacterial dissemination. Manifestations include:

- diffuse skin thickening
- plaque-like hyperpigmentation
- leonine fasciitis
- paresis
- regional anaesthesia
- testicular destruction
- blindness


 it grows best at temperatures below core body temperatures, explaining why it manifests in the skin, superficial nerves (specifically Schwann cells), eyes, and testes

# Cardiac Response to Exercise

Lusitropy = myocardial relaxation

Increased with exercise 

Contributes to increased preload 

Key regulatory protein - Phospholamban
- Inibits sarcoplasmic reticulum Ca2+ ATPase
- Phospholamban is phosphorylated via beta adrenergic stimulation
- Stops inhibiting SERCA
- Which causes SERCA to take up calcium leading to relaxation

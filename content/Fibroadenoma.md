# Fibroadenoma

Tumour of fibrous tissue and glands (Fig. 16.3). Develops from a whole lobule

Most common benign neoplasm of the breast; usually seen in premenopausal women

Presents as a well-circumscribed, mobile marble-like mass. 'Breast mouse'

==Oestrogen sensitive- grows during pregnancy and may be painful during the menstrual cycle==

==Benign, with no increased risk of carcinoma==

If >3cm surgical excision

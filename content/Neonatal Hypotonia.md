# Neonatal Hypotonia

Causes of neonatal hypotonia include:  

- neonatal sepsis [[Neonatal Sepsis]]
- Werdnig-Hoffman disease (spinal muscular atrophy type 1) [[Werdnig Hoffman Disease]]
- [[Hypothyroidism]]
- Prader-Willi  [[Prader-Willi Syndrome]]

Maternal causes  

- maternal drugs e.g. benzodiazepines
- maternal myasthenia gravis [[Myasthenia Gravis]]

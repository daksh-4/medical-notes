# Lambert-Eaton Syndrome

1. Antibodies against presynaptic calcium channels of the neuromuscular junction
    
2. Arises as a paraneoplastic syndrome, most commonly due to small cell carcinoma of
    
    the lung
    
3. Leads to impaired acetylcholine release

1. Firing of presynaptic calcium channels is required for acetylcholine release. D. Clinical features

1. Proximal muscle weakness that improves with use; eyes are usually spared.
    
2. Anticholinesterase agents do not improve symptoms.
    
3. Resolves with resection of the cancer

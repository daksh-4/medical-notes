# Acetylcholine

![[Pasted image 20241105103538.png|500]]

Major NT for parasympathetic system also important for sweat glands and voluntary skeletal muscle contraction 

Nicotinic receptors
- Activation - spasm/fasciculation
- Blockade - paralysis

Cholinergic toxidrome - symptoms when you activate all cholinergic receptors
- Classic cause - organophosphates
- Inhibit AChE which normally breaks ACh into Choline and Acetate
- Increase GI motility
	- Nausea, vomiting, cramps, diarrhoea
- Secretory glands
	- Sweating, salivation and lacrimation
- Bladder 
	- Detrusor contraction - urination
- Diarrhoea, drooling, incontinence
- Heart
	- Decreased contractility and heart rate
- Lungs
	- Bronchoconstriction
	- Wheezing, dyspnoea, flare of asthma/COPD
- Bradycardia and wheezing
- Pinpoint pupil
- Muscle twitching
- CNS acetylcholine receptors 
	- Confusing, lethargy and seizures
- Treatment
	- Atropine - muscarinic antagonist 
	- Pralidoxime - regenerates AChE
		- Binds AChE
		- Displaces organophosphates

ACh Receptor Agonists - bethanechol, carbachol, methacholine, pilocarpine
- Only tertiary amines can cross the [[Blood Brain Barrier]]

Bethanechol
- Similar structure to ACh
- NH2 group - strong resistance to AChE
- Also has a beta methyl group - reduced nicotinic activity so it doesn't effect sketal muscle
- Muscarinic activity only
- Treatment of ileus and urinary retention

Carbachol
- NH2 group
- Active at nicotinic and muscarinic receptors 
- Used topically for pupillary constriction - glaucoma 

Methacholine
- Some AChE resistance due to beta methyl group 
- Reduced activity at nicotinic receptos
- Asthma diagnosis

Pilocarpine 
- Stimulates nicotine and muscarinic receptors
- Tertiary amine can cross [[Blood Brain Barrier]]
	- May cause seizures
- Glaucoma
- Sjogren's - dry mouth 
- Sweat test for CF

Another way to increase Acetylcholine activity is Cholinesterase inhibitors
- Can activate both muscarinic and nicotinic receptors

![[Pasted image 20241105104925.png]]

Neostigmine is a quaternary amine

Physostigmine is a tertiary so can cross the BBB. 

Donepezil 
- Tertiary amine
- Treatment for Alzheimer's because it is selective for synapses in the brain

Cholinergic medications can worsen COPD or ulcers
- Bronchoconstriction - COPD flare
- increased gastric acid - ulcers

### Anticholinergic Toxidrome

Opposite so blocking of cholinergic receptors

Drugs
- Antihistamines
- TCAs
- Atropine
- Jimson weed (Datura stramonium) 

Features
- Dry skin - blockade of sympathetic sweat glands
- Hyperthermia - loss of sweating
- Flushing due to reflex vasodilation in response to hyperthermia
- Dry mouth and eyes
- Dilated pupils (mydriasis)
- Loss of lens accommodation -> blurry vision
- Delirium from blockade of central ACh

Treatment 
- Physostigmine
- AChE inhibitor
- Tertiary amine that crosses BBB 
- Improves ACh levels in the body

Atropine
- Classic muscarinic antagonist
- Overdose causes anticholinergic toxidrome

Scopolamine
- Treatment for motion sickness
	- Over activity of acetylcholine an dhistamine in the brain
- Patfch blocks M1 receptors
- Antihistamines: meclizine, diemenhydrinate
- Side effects
	- Dry mouth
	- Urinary retention
	- Constipation

Anticholinergics used for mydriasis - normally tropicamide 

Anticholinergics sometimes used to treat Parkinson's 
- When dopamine is depleted, there is a relative excess of ACh
- Benztropine and trihexyphenidyl

Overactive bladder
- Anticholinergics inhibit the detrusor muscle function
- Oxybutynin
- Solifenacin
- Tolterodine

COPD drugs
- Ipratropium and tiotropium
- Glycopyrrolate for airway secretions

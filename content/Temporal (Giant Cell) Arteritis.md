# Temporal (Giant Cell) Arteritis

Large vessel

Most common form of [[Vasculitis]] in older adults above 50 usually affects females

Branches of the carotid artery
- Headache (temporal)
- Visual disturbances (ophthalmic)
- Jaw claudication
- Flu like symptoms with joint and muscle pain (polymyalgia rheumatica present in a subset of temporal arteritis)
- ESR is elevated

Biopsy
- Inflamed vessel wall with giant cells (granulomatous inflammation) and intimal fibrosis. 
- Negative biopsy does not exclude disease

![[Pasted image 20240913154610.png]]

Pink fibrosis between the intima and the media. This results in the narrowing of the lumen. The cell on the middle of the left edge is a giant cell because it has multiple nuclei

Treatment
- Corticosteroids
- Treat quickly due to high risk of blindness by thrombosis of the ophthalmic artery.

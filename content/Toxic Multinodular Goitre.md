# Toxic Multinodular Goitre

Enlarged thyroid gland with multiple nodules 
- Nodules usually contain mutated TSH receptor
- Do not respond to TSH 

Findings 
- Palpable nodule
- Hyperthyroidism symptoms/labs

Take up I131 in radioactive iodine uptake leading to 'hot' nodule. If the nodule is 'cold' more likely to be cancerous
    
Due to relative iodine deficiency
    
Usually nontoxic (euthyroid)
    
Rarely, regions become TSH-independent leading to T4 release and hyperthyroidism  ('toxic goiter').

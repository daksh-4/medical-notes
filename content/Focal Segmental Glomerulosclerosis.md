# Focal Segmental Glomerulosclerosis

A. Most common cause of nephrotic syndrome in Hispanics and African Americans

Usually idiopathic; may be associated with HIV, heroin use, and sickle cell disease

Focal (some glomeruli) and segmental (involving only part of the glomerulus) sclerosis on H&E stain (Fig. 12.8). Dense pink deposition of collagen in the glomeruli. 

Effacement of foot processes on EM

No immune complex deposits; negative IF

Poor response to steroids; progresses to chronic renal failure

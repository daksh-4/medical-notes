# Disorders of Fibrinolysis

Due to plasmin overactivity resulting in excessive cleavage of fibrinogen. 

tPA (tissue plasminogen activator) normally converts plasminogen to plasmin, which breaks down fibrin and fibrinogen. 

Alpha 2 antiplasmin is a molecule that inactivates plasmin

Radical prostatectomy - release of urokinase which activates plasmin

Cirrhosis of the liver - reduced production of alpha 2 antiplasmin. 

Presents similar to DIC. 

Lab findings
- Increased PT and PTT 
- Increased bleeding time with NORMAL PLATELET COUNT
- Increased fibrinogen split products WITHOUT D-DIMERS (this is because no fibrin clots are being formed)

Treatment 
Aminocaprioc acid; blocks activation of plasminogen

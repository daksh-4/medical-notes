# Myeloproliferative Disorders

If there is a proliferation of mature cells in the myeloid lineage then it is called a myeloproliferative disorder. 

Disease of late adulthood

Results in a high WBC count with hypercellular bone marrow. 

Cells of all myeloid lineages are increased but classified on the dominant cells. 

Complications
- Increased risk for hyperuricaemia and gout due to high turnover of cells and activation of the purine degradation pathway to remove nuclear materials. 
- Progression to marrow fibrosis (spent phase)
- Many can transform to acute leukaemia

# Subacute Sclerosing Panencephalitis

Progressive, debilitating encephalitis leading to death

Due to slowly progressing, persistent infection of the brain by measles virus. 

Infection occurs in infancy; neurologic signs arise years later (during childhood). C. Characterized by viral inclusions within neurons (gray matter) and oligodendrocytes (white matter)

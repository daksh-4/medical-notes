# Pleomorphic Adenoma

Pleomorphic means multiple tissue types are present

Benign tumour composed of stromal (e.g. cartilage) and epithelial tissue (e.g. glands)

Most common tumour of the salivary gland

Usually arises in the parotid

Clinical features
- gradual onset painless unilateral swelling of the parotid gland
- typically movable on examination rather than fixed
- Mobile painless circumscribed mass at the angle of the jaw
	- Mobile because it hasn't invaded local tissue
	- Painless because it hasn't invaded the facial nerve

High rate of recurrence
- Extension of small islands of tumour through the tumour capsule often results in incomplete resection
- Irregular margins 

Rarely may transform into carcinoma if not removed via surgical excision
- Presents with signs of facial nerve damage

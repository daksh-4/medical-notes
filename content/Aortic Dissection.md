What is the most common cause of death in patients with aortic dissection?
# Aortic Dissection

Intimal tear with dissection of blood through the media of the aortic wall 

This requires tension on the wall and weakness in the media layer

Tension is high in the proximal aorta near the aortic valve

Occurs in the proximal 10cm of the aorta with pre-existing weakness of the media.
- In the proximal 10cm, the wall is very thick and the diffusion of oxygen across the wall is not enough to supply it
- So you have vaso vasorum which supply the outer half. 
- Hypertension results in hyaline arteriolosclerosis of these vessels, decreasing the calibre of the lumen
- Decreased blood flow leads to atrophy of the smooth muscle
- Weakening of the media results in dissection

Risk factors
- Hypertension number 1
- Atherosclerosis 
- Marfans + Ehlers Danlos
- Bicuspid Aortic Valve
- Turner Syndrome (due to bicuspid and coarctation)
- Tertiary syphilis due to aortitis

Most common cause is HTN also associated with inherited defects of connective tissue

Complication is pericardial tamponade - most common cause of death
- Dissection goes backwards and causes filling of the pericardium

Propogation to aortic arch
- Stroke
- Horner's syndrome
- Vocal cord paralysis

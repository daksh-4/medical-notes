# Kawasaki Disease

Medium vessel

Asian children less than the age of four

Presentation
- Fever
- Conjunctivitis
- Erythematous rash of palms and soles that peels
	- Kid sitting on a Kawasaki motorcycle (feet and hands and high heart rate)
- Strawberry tongue
- Bright red, cracked lips
- Enlarged cervical lymph nodes

Coronary artery involvement is common
- Thrombosis with myocardial infarction
- ==Aneurysm with rupture==

Treatment 
- Aspirin and IVIG
	- You normally never give a child with a viral illness aspirin - Reyes syndrome encephalopathy and massive liver necrosis 
	- Aspirin stops platelet aggregation by inhibiting platelet cyclooxygenase
	- IVIG binds to circulating antibodies and antigens reducing the formation of complexes which exacerbate the inflammatory process
- Disease is self-limited

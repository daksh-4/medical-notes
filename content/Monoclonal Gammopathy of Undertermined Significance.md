# Monoclonal Gammopathy of Undertermined Significance

MGUS - myoclonal gammopathy of undertermined significance
- Isolated M spike with no other symptoms of [[Multiple Myeloma]]
- Seen in 5% of 70 year old individuals
- 1% of patients with MGUS develop [[Multiple Myeloma]] each year

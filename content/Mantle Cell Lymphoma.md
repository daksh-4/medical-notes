# Mantle Cell Lymphoma

Neoplastic small B cells (CD20+) that expand the mantle zone (region adjacent to the follicle)
Clinically presents in late adulthood with painless LAD

Driven by t(11;14)
Cyclin D1 on chromosome 11 translocates to the Ig heavy chain locus on chromosome 14
Overexpression of cyclin D1 promotes G1/S transition in the cell cycle. Cyclin D1 phosphorylates the regulators allowing it to move through the cell cycle.

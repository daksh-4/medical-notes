# Waldenstrom Macroglobulinaemia

B-cell lymphoma with monoclonal IgM production
Called macro because IgM is a large pentamer

Clinical features
- Generalised lympadenopathy but no lytic bone lesions
- Increased serum proteins with M spike
- Visual and neurological deficits (retinal haemorrhage or [[Stroke]])
- Bleeding

Acute complications are treated with plasmapheresis
- Removes IgM from the serum

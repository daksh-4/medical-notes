# Infectious Arthritis

1. Arthritis due to an infectious agent, usually bacterial
    
2. Causes include
    
    1. N gonorrhoeae-young adults; most common cause
        
    2. S aureus-older children and adults; 2nd most common cause
        
3. Classically involves a single joint, usually the knee
    
4. Presents as a warm joint with limited range of motion; fever, increased white count,
    
    and elevated ESR are often present.

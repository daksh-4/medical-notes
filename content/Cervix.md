# Cervix

1. Anatomically, comprises the "neck" of the uterus
    
2. Divided into the exocervix (visible on vaginal exam) and endocervix
    
    1. Exocervix is lined by nonkeratinizing squamous epithelium.
        
    2. Endocervix is lined by a single layer of columnar cells.
        
    3. Junction between the exocervix and endocervix is called the transformation
        
        zone (Fig. 13.4A).

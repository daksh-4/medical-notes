# Poliomyelitis

Damage to the anterior motor horn due to poliovirus infection  

Presents with lower motor neurone signs- flaccid paralysis with muscle atrophy, fasciculations, weakness with decreased muscle tone, impaired reflexes, and negative Babinski sign (downgoing toes)

- ﻿﻿Classic presentation
- ﻿﻿Unvaccinated child
- ﻿﻿Febrile illness
- ﻿﻿Neuro symptoms 4-5 days later
- ﻿﻿Weakness (legs>arms)
- ﻿﻿Flaccid muscle tone

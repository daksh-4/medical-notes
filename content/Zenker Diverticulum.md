# Zenker Diverticulum

Outpouching of pharyngeal mucosa through acquired defect in the muscular wall 

Arises above upper oesophageal sphincter at junction of the oesophagus and pharynx

Presents with dysphagia, obstruction and halitosis

Known as a false diverticulum because only one part of the wall (mucosa) goes through

[[Fat Metabolism]] [[Endocrinology]]

A patient with a L-CAT deficiency results in less maturation in HDLs. HDLs can still collect free cholesterol, but they can't efficiently esterify it. 

Normally, cholesterol esters move into the HDL core, allowing HDL to mature from a small discoid particle into a larger spherical HDL. Without LCAT, it remains immature, and reverse cholesterol transfer is impaired.

# Cardiac Tumours

### Myxoma

Usually occur in the left atrium attached to the atrial septum at the border of the fossa ovalis. They are benign tumours

Contain mesenchymal cells, endothelial cells, clots, mucopolysaccharides

Often have system symptoms
- Fevers, chills and sweats
- Can develop a stroke

If they are big enough they can disrupt mitral valve function
- Regurgitation and heart failure symptoms

Sometimes they sit in the mitral valve
- "Ball in valve"
- Mitral stenosis symptoms due to obstruction
- Syncope or sudden death
- Auscultation you hear diastolic tumour plop

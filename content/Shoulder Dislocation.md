# Shoulder Dislocation

Shoulder dislocation 

Shoulder dislocations occur when the humeral head dislodges from the glenoid cavity of the scapula. The shoulder is the most common joint in the body to dislocate, accounting for approximately 50% of all major joint dislocations.  
  
Anterior shoulder dislocations account for > 95% of cases.  
  
There are a large number of different techniques for reducing shoulders. There is limited evidence that one is better than another. If the dislocation is recent then reduction may be attempted without any analgesia/sedation. However, other patients may require analgesia +/- sedation to ensure the rotator cuff muscles are relaxed.

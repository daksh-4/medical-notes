# Tumour Lysis Syndrome

Release of phosphate, nucleic acids and inflammatory mediators

Phosphate forms calcium phosphate crystals and nucleic acid breaks down into uric acid crystals - these cause injury in the renal tubules

Soluble uric acid and inflammatory mediators leads to oxygen radicals and inflammation

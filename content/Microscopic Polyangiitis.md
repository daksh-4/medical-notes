# Microscopic Polyangiitis

Small vessel

Necrotising [[Vasculitis]] involving multiple organs especially lung and kidney
Similar to Wegener's granulomatosis but nasopharyngeal involvement and granulomas are absent. 
Serum p-ANCA levels correlate with disease activity

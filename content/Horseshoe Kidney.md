# Horseshoe Kidney

1. Conjoined kidneys usually connected at the lower pole (Fig. 12.1); most common congenital renal anomaly
    
2. Kidney is abnormally located in the lower abdomen; horseshoe kidney gets caught on the inferior mesenteric artery root during its ascent from the pelvis to the abdomen .

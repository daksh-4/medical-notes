# Vaccination Schedule

| **Age**      | **Recommended immunisations**                                                                                           |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| At birth     | BCG if risk factors (see below)                                                                                         |
| 2 months     | '6-1 vaccine' (diphtheria, tetanus, whooping cough, polio, Hib and hepatitis B)  <br>Oral rotavirus vaccine   <br>Men B |
| 3 months*    | '6-1 vaccine' (diphtheria, tetanus, whooping cough, polio, Hib and hepatitis B)  <br>Oral rotavirus vaccine   <br>PCV   |
| 4 months     | '6-1 vaccine' (diphtheria, tetanus, whooping cough, polio, Hib and hepatitis B)  <br>Men B                              |
| 12-13 months | Hib/Men C  <br>MMR  <br>PCV  <br>Men B                                                                                  |
| 2-8 years    | Flu vaccine (annual)                                                                                                    |
| 3-4 years    | '4-in-1 pre-school booster' (diphtheria, tetanus, whooping cough and polio)  <br>MMR                                    |
| 12-13 years  | HPV vaccination                                                                                                         |
| 13-18 years  | '3-in-1 teenage booster' (tetanus, diphtheria and polio)  <br>Men ACWY                                                  |

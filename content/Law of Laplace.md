# Law of Laplace

![[Pasted image 20241102120613.png]]

Pressure generated by left ventricular contraction x radius of left ventricle divided by wall thickness. 

Afterload increases pressure in the left ventricle
- Hypertension and aortic stenosis 
- This leads to increased wall tension
- Pressure overload

Preload increases radius 
- Chronic valvular disease aortic or mitral regurg
- Volume overload 

Hypertrophy 
- Decreases wall tension by increasing thickness
- Force distributed over mass 
- Occurs with chronic pressure or volume overload

# Anaemia of Chronic Disease

Why do patients with chronic inflammatory state get iron deficiency?
Anaemia of chronic disease because iron is locked away inside the macrophages
Chronic disease results in acute phase reactants. 
Hepcidin sequesters iron in storage sites which limits iron transfer from macrophages to erythroid precursors. 
Hepcidin also suppresses EPO production. 

Lab findings 
- Increased ferritin and decreased TIBC
- Decreased serum iron and decreased percentage saturation
- Increased FEP (free erythrocyte protoporphyrin)

Initially starts as a normocytic anaemia and then becomes microcytic 

Treatment
- Treat the underlying disease
- Exogenous EPO is useful in a subset of patients, especially those with cancer

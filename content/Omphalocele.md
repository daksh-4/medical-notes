# Omphalocele

Persistent herniation of bowel into umbilical cord  

Due to failure of herniated intestines (that were in the umbilicum) to return to the body cavity during development (rotation of the bowel during embryological development)

Contents are covered by peritoneum and amnion of the umbilical cord

# AV Blocks

AV node disease
- Usually less dangerous
- Conduction improves with exertion (symptathetic activity can speed conduction through the AV node)
HIS-Purkinje disease
- Usually does not improve 
- Often progresses to complete heart block 
- Pacemaker

AV blocks

Type 1
- PR prolongation only >200ms or one big box
- Usually in the AV node
- Can be caused by slowed AV nodal conduction via beta blockers or calcium channel blockers or well-trained athletes (due to high parasympathetic tone at rest)

Type 2
- Mobitz 1
	- Progressive PR prolongation 
	- Similar causes as 1st degree AV block
- Mobitz 2
	- Usually in the HIS purkinje system - only able to conduct sometimes leading to dropped beats
	- Often seen with bundle branch block 
	- Symptomatic

Type 3
- No conduction
- Usually in the HIS Purkinje system 
- Regular RR intervals excludes Wenkebach

In v tach you also get dissociation of p waves, you can see them as small bumps on the ecg.

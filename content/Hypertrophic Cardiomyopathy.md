# Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy

Due to genetic mutations in sarcomere proteins most commonly autosomal dominant but there is variable expression. Family members have different severity and location of hypertrophy. 

Often single-point missense mutations 
- Point mutation leading to an altered amino acid in protein
- Often involving the cardiac sarcomere proteins
	- Beta myosin heavy chain
	- Myosin binding proteins

Clinical features
- Many are asymptomatic 
- Decreased cardiac output due to diastolic dysfunction (heart failure_
- Difficulty emptying due to LVOT obstruction
- Chest pain
- Sudden death due to ventricular arrhythmias common cause of sudden death in young athletes
- Mitral regurgitation due to lots of blood in the heart
- Syncope with exercise due to functional aortic stenosis by the excess muscle

Maneuvers to accentuate HCM mumurs (sounds like aortic stenosis)
- Valsalva
- Increased thoracic pressure -> compression of veins -> low venous return -> less preload -> smaller LV cavity
- Obstructing septum moves further into the outflow tract so the murmur increases in intensity
- Squatting does the opposite as blood volume stored in the legs returns to the heart.

Associations
- Maternal diabetes - lots of sugar and fuel to the heart making it extra thick, but normally resolves
- Friedreich Ataxia - trinucleotide autosomal recessive. Spinocerebellar symptoms

Biopsy
- Myofiber hypertrophy with disarray, hypertrophy and interstitial fibrosis.
- You can tell there is dissarray because the myocytes aren't nicely lined up.

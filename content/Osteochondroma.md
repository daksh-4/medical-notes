# Osteochondroma

THE MOST COMMON BENIGN BONE TUMOUR

Males younger than 25

Location - Metaphysis

Sticky outy bits on the metaphysis, covered by cartilage cap (chon means cartilage). LATERAL PROJECTION GROWTH

Bone is continuous with the marrow space 

Overlying cartilage can transform (rarely) to chondrosarcoma

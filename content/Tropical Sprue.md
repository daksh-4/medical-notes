# Tropical Sprue

Damage to small bowel villi due to an unknown organism resulting in malabsorption

Similar to celiac disease except:
- Occurs in tropical regions (e.g., Caribbean)
- Arises after infectious diarrhoea and responds to antibiotics
- Damage is most prominent in jejunum (folate) and ileum (vitamin B12) (secondary vitamin B12 or folate deficiency may ensue); duodenum is less commonly involved.

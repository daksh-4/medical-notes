# Rhinitis

Inflammation of the nasal mucosa

Adenovirus is the most common cause

Sneezing, congestion and runny nose

Allergic Rhinitis
- Due to type 1 hypersensitivity to pollen for example
- Characterised by inflammatory infiltrate with eosinophils
- Associated with asthma and eczema 

Nasal Polyp
- Protrusion of edematous, inflamed nasal mucosa
- Usually due to repeated bouts of rhinitis
- Also occurs in ==cystic fibrosis== and aspirin-intolerant asthma (Samter's triad)

# Duodenal Atresia

Atresia means ending in a blind loop

Congenital failure of duodenum to canalize; associated with [[Down's Syndrome]]
Clinical features
- Polyhydramnios - baby swallows and digests amniotic fluid
- Distension of stomach and blind loop of duodenum ('double bubble' sign, Fig.10.15)
	- The pyloric sphincter doesn't distend which results in the separation of the bubbles
	- ![[Pasted image 20240930153617.png|500]]
- Bilious vomiting

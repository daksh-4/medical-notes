# Henoch Schonlein Purpura

Small vessel

[[Vasculitis]] due to IgA immune complex deposition
Most common [[Vasculitis]] in children

Presents with 
- Palpable purpura on buttocks and legs
- GI pain and bleeding
- Haematuria (IgA nephropathy IgA deposits in the mesangium leading to glomerular bleeding)
- Usually occurs following an URTI
	- IgA antibodies made during infection which deposits causing the [[Vasculitis]]

Self limited may recur but treat with steroids if severe

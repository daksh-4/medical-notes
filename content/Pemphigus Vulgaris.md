# Pemphigus Vulgaris

Autoimmune destruction of desmosomes between keratinocytes

Due to IgG antibody against desmoglein (type II hypersensitivity)
- This disrupts connections in the stratum spinosum
- ![[Pasted image 20241103102750.png]]

Presents as skin and oral mucosa bullae (Fig. l9.4A) with positive Nikolsky

Acantholysis
- Loss of connections between keratinocytes
- Often loss of desmosomes 
- Rounded freely floating keratinocytes in the epidermis which can result in suprabasal blisters

Basal layer cells remain attached to basement membrane via hemidesinosomes ('tombstone' appearance, Fig. l9.4B).
    
Thin-walled bullae rupture easily (Nikolsky sign), leading to shallow erosions with dried crust.
    
Immunofluorescence highlights IgG surrounding keratinocytes in a 'fish net' pattern.

Treatment
- Immunosuppresants
- Prone to infections

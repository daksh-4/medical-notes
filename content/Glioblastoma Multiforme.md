# Glioblastoma Multiforme

Malignant, high-grade tumour of astrocytes

Most common primary malignant CNS tumor in adults

Usually arises in the cerebral hemisphere; characteristically crosses the corpus callosum ('butterfly' lesion, Fig. 17.16A}

Characterized by regions of necrosis surrounded by tumour cells (pseudopalisading, Fig. 17.16B} and endothelial cell proliferation; tumour cells are GFAP positive. GFAP is the intermediate filament present in glial cells 

Poor prognosis

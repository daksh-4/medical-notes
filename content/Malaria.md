# Malaria

Infection of RBCs and liver with Plasmodium. Transmitted by female Anopheles

![[Pasted image 20240910183107.png]]

RBCs rupture as part of the Plasmodium life cycle. 
Results in intravascular haemolysis and cyclical fever. 

Falciparum - daily fever
Vivax and ovale - fever every other day

Spleen also consumes some infected RBCs
- Results in mild extravascular haemolysis with splenomegaly

[[Endocrinology]]

Niacin binds to a receptor, GPR109A, on adipocytes, which inhibits hormone-sensitive lipase. Decreased hormone-sensitive lipase activity decreases the release of fatty acids from adipose tissue, decreasing fatty acid delivery to the liver, which in turn decreases hepatic triglyceride synthesis and decreases VLDL secretion. 

There is another mechanism through which niacin reduces hepatic uptake and degradation of ApoA1. ApoA1 stays in circulation longer. HDL particles survive longer, and plasma HDL concentration increases.

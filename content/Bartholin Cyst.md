# Bartholin Cyst

A. Cystic dilation of the Bartholin gland

One Bartholin gland is present on each side of the vaginal canal and produces mucus-like fluid that drains via ducts into the lower vestibule.

B. Arises due to inflammation and obstruction of gland 1. Usually occurs in women of reproductive age related to infection and STIs

C. Presents as a unilateral, painful cystic lesion at the lower vestibule adjacent to the vaginal canal

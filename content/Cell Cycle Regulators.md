Burkitt Lymphoma - B cell tumour. B cells express immunoglobulin heavy chain which sits on chromosome 14. If the patient gets a translocation with the myc gene on chromosome 8, there will be a switch between myc and the IgH gene. This will turn myc on, leading to massive overproduction, leading to massive growth of the tumour.

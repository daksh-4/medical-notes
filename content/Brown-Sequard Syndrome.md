# Brown-Sequard Syndrome

- ﻿﻿Loss of half of spinal cord
- ﻿﻿Trauma or tumor
- ﻿﻿Lose pain/temp contralateral side
- ﻿﻿Lose motor, position, vibration ipsilateral side
- If the lesion is above T1, may present with ipsilateral Horner syndrome

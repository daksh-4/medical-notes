# McCune Albright Syndrome

McCune-Albright syndrome is not inherited, it is due to a random, somatic mutation in the GNAS gene.  
  
Features  

- precocious puberty
- cafe-au-lait spots
- polyostotic fibrous dysplasia - bone is replaced by fibrous tissue leading to increased risk of fractures and deformities
- short stature

# Pneumothorax

Spontaneous pneumothorax (subtype of pneumothorax)
- Rupture of emphysematous bleb; seen in young adults. 
- Results in collapse of portion of the lung
- Trachea shifts to the side of the collapse

Tension pneumothorax
- Penetrating chest wall injury means air can come in, but air can't escape.
- This leads to increased air in the pleural space which compresses the lung and other structures

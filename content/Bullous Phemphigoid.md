# Bullous Phemphigoid

Autoimmune destruction of hemidesmosomes between basal cells and the underlying basement membrane
    
Due to IgG antibody against basement membrane collagen
    
Presents as blisters of the skin (Fig. l9.5A); oral mucosa is spared.

Basal cell layer is detached from the basement membrane (Fig. l9.5B).

Tense bullae do not rupture easily; clinically milder than pemphigus vulgaris 

Immunofluorescence highlights IgG along basement membrane (linear pattern).

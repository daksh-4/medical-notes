[[Genetics]]

Northern - mRNA. A sample containing a large number of mRNA molecules is separated via gel electrophoresis. Separated bands are then transferred to a membrane and hybridized with a probe containing a nucleotide sequence complementary to the mRNA of interest. 

Western - detect a target polypeptide or protein from a mixed sample. First separate by gel electrophoresis. Then probed with a primary antibody specific for the protein of interest. Then washed and treated with a secondary antibody that binds to the primary one and can be detected.

Southern - DNA sequences. DNA fragements and radiolabeled DNA probe. Restriction site mutations can be detected because they alter DNA fragment lengths

Southwestern - DNA binding proteins

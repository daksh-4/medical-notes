# Achalasia

Disordered oesophageal motility with inability to relax the lower sphincter

Due to damaged ganglion cells in the myenteric plexus

Damage can be idiopathic or secondary to known insult like Chagas disease

Dysphagia for solids and liquids!

Massive dilatation of the oesophageal wall - bird-beak sign

![[Pasted image 20240929131048.png|500]]

Clinical features
- Dysphagia for solids and liquids
- Putrid breath
- High lower oesophageal sphincter pressure on oesophageal manometry
- Increased risk for squamous cell carcinoma of the oesophagus

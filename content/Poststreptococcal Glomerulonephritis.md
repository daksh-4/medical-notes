# Poststreptococcal Glomerulonephritis

1. Nephritic syndrome that arises after group A beta haemolytic streptococcal infection of the skin (impetigo) or pharynx
    
    1. Occurs with nephritogenic strains (which carry the M protein virulence factor)
        
    2. May occur after infection with nonstreptococcal organisms as well
        
2. Presents 2-3 weeks after infection as hematuria (cola-colored urine), oliguria, hypertension, and periorbital edema

l. Usually seen in children, but may occur in adults

C. Hypercellular, inflamed glomeruli on H&E

4. Mediated by immune complex deposition (granular IF); subepithelial 'humps' on
    
    EM (Fig. 12.13)
    
5. Treatment is supportive.

l. Children rarely (1%) progress to renal failure.  
2. Some adults (25%) develop [[Rapidly Progressive Glomerulonephritis]]

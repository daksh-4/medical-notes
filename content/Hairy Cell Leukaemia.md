# Hairy Cell Leukaemia

Hairy cell leukaemia

Also a neoplastic proliferation of mature B cells
Characterised by hairy cytoplasmic processes. 
Cells are positive for TRAP (tartrate resistance acid phosphatase). This is a stain

Clinical features
- Splenomegaly of the red pulp 
	- Splenomegaly normally happens with a lot of chronic leukaemias but you would expect the hyperplasia to be in the white pulp area. 
	- In Hairy cell it is in the red pulp
- Dry tap with bone marrow aspiration
	- Because the bone marrow is fibrosed
- Lymphadenopathy is usually absent

Cells are TRAP(ped) in the spleen and the bone marrow so can't go to the lymph nodes. 

Excellent response to 2-CDA 
- Adenosine deaminase inhibitor
	- Part of the purine pathway
- Adenosine accumulates to toxic levels in neoplastic B cells

# CNS Aneurysms

- ﻿﻿Saccular or Berry
- ﻿﻿More common type

- ﻿﻿Charcot-Bouchard aneurysms
- ﻿﻿Microaneurysm
- ﻿﻿Cause of hemorrhagic stroke in HTN
- Usually a branch of the lenticulostriate artery
- ﻿﻿Severe HTN
- ﻿﻿Similar: lacunar strokes

Berry aneurysm association
- ADPKD
- Ehlers-Danlos
- Coarcation of the Aorta
- Older age
- Hypertension
- Smoking

They like to occur at branch points

![[Pasted image 20241107194058.png]]

Asymptomatic but sometimes you can get symptoms
- Headache
- Visual field defects
	- Bitemporal hemianopia if compressing the optic chiasm
	- Anterior communicating
- CNIII palsy and eye pain with a unilateral headache
	- down and out
	- ptosis
	- dilated pupil posterior communicating
		- if the pupil is spared then it is a lesion not an aneurysm
		- if spared often associated with diabetes due to ischaemic neuropathy of CNIII

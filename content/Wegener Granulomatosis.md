# Wegener Granulomatosis

Small vessel

Necrotising granulomatous [[Vasculitis]] involving the nasopharynx, lungs and kidneys

cANCA

Treatment
Cyclophosphamide

Presents in middle aged males
- sinusitis or nasopharyngeal ulceration
- haemoptysis with bilateral nodular lung infiltrates
- Haematuria due to rapidly progressive glomerulonephritis
- Serum c-ANCA levels correlate with disease activity

Large necrotising granulomas with adjacent necrotising [[Vasculitis]]. Giant cells and epitheloid histiocytes

Treatment 
Cyclophosphamide and steroids
Relapses are common

# Membranoproliferative Glomerulonephritis

Thick glomerular basement membrane on H&E, often with 'tram-track' appearance. 

Cytoplasm of the mesangial cell proliferates and separates the deposits creating the tram track appearance

Due to immune complex deposition (granular IF)
Divided into two types based on location of deposits

Type I- subendothelial (Fig. 12.10); associated with HBV and HCV

Type II (dense deposit disease)-intramembranous in the basement membrane; associated with C3 nephritic factor (autoantibody that stabilizes C3 convertase, leading to overactivation of complement, inflammation, and low levels of circulating C3)

Can produce nephrotic syndrome, nephritic syndrome or both

Poor response to steroids; progresses to chronic renal failure

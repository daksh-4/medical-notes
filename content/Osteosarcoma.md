# Osteosarcoma

A. Malignant proliferation of osteoblasts

Peak incidence is seen in teenagers; less commonly seen in the elderly
- Risk factors include familial retinoblastoma, Paget disease, and radiation exposure. [[Paget's Disease of Bone]]
- Arises in the metaphysis of long bones, usually the distal femur or proximal tibia (region of the knee)

 Presents as a pathologic fracture or bone pain with swelling

Imaging reveals a destructive mass with a 'sunburst' appearance and lifting of the periosteum (Codman angle).

Biopsy reveals pleomorphic cells (large malignant cells) that produce osteoid

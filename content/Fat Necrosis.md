# Fat Necrosis

==Inflammation and hard, irregular breast lump==

Necrosis of breast fat

Could be due to enzymatic destruction of fat. 

Usually related to trauma; however, a history of trauma may not always be evident.

Presents as a mass on physical exam or abnormal calcification on mammography (due to saponification). This is because of some fibrosis and giant cell inflammation. 

Biopsy shows necrotic fat with associated calcifications and giant cells.

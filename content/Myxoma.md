# Myxoma

Benign mesenchymal proliferation with a gelatinous appearance these are the only non mature cells in the heart

Abundant ground substance on histology

Most common primary cardiac tumour in adults

Pedunculated mass in the left atrium 
- Causes syncope due to obstruction of mitral valve

# Diffuse Large B Cell Lymphoma

Neoplastic large B-cells (CD20+) that grow diffusely in sheets. 
Most common form of Non-Hodgkin's lymphoma
Clinically aggressive because it is poorly-differentiated

Arises sporadically or from transformation of follicular lymphoma. 
Presents in late adulthood as an enlarging lymph node or an extranodal mass

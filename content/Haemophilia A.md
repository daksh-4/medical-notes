# Haemophilia A

Genetic FVIII deficiency. Haemophilia "eight" sounds like a 

X linked recessive - predominantly affects males
Can arise from a de novo mutation without any family history. 

Presents with deep tissue, joint and postsurgical bleeding. 

### Lab findings
Increased PTT and normal PT
Decreased FVIII
Normal platelet count and bleeding time

Treatment
Recombinant factor 8

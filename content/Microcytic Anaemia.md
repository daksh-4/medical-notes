# Microcytic Anaemia

Red blood cells are made from erythroblasts. It keeps dividing to form RBCs. With each division the cells become smaller. 

Microcytosis is due to an "extra" division of the erythroblast. This is because there is DECREASED PRODUCTION OF HAEMOGLOBIN. 

Red blood cells try to maintain the concentration of haemoglobin in the cell by becoming smaller. 

Haemoglobin - haeme and globin 
Haeme is composed of iron and protoporphyrin. 

4 MAIN CAUSES OF MICROCYTIC ANAEMIA 
Iron deficiency, [[Anaemia of Chronic Disease]], sideroblastic anaemia (low protoporphyrin), thalassaemia (decreased production of globin)

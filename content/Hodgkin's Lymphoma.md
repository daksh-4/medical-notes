# Hodgkin's Lymphoma

NHL has an entire mass of malignant cells. Whereas in HL, you have rare neoplastic cells called Reed-Sternberg cells which draw in other cells by secreting cytokines. 

Reed-Sternberg cells
- Large B cell with multilobed nuclei and prominent nucleoli
- CD15+ and CD30+ 

![[Pasted image 20240912160101.png]]

Reed-Sternberg cells secrete cytokines
- Occasionally results in B symptoms (fevers, chills and night sweats)
- Attract reactive lymphocytes, plasma cells, macrophages and eosinophils which 
	- Also form the basis for classification
- may lead to fibrosis.

HL subtypes
- Nodular sclerosis (70% of cases)
	- Enlarging cervical neck or mediastinal lymph node in a young adult usually female
	- RS cells are present in lake like spaces (lacunar cells are a special kind of RS cell shown as white blob)
	- ![[Pasted image 20240912160705.png]]
	- Lymph node is divided by bands of fibrosis shown in pink
	- ![[Pasted image 20240912160542.png]]
- Lymphocyte rich
	- Best prognosis
- Mixed cellularity 
	- Is associated with abundant eosinophils which are called in by IL-5
- Lymphocyte depleted
	- Worst prognosis seen in elderly and in HIV+

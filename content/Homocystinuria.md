# Homocystinuria

Homocystinuria is a rare autosomal recessive disease caused by a deficiency of cystathionine beta synthase. This results in severe elevations in plasma and urine homocysteine concentrations.  
  
Features  

- often patients have fine, fair hair - homocysteine disrupts the sulfur containing compounds which are crucial for normal keratin and melanin production
- musculoskeletal - interference with collagen cross linking
    - Marfanoid body habitus: arachnodactyly etc
    - osteoporosis
    - kyphosis
- neurological: may have learning difficulties, seizures - homocysteine is neurotoxic
- ocular
    - downwards (inferonasal) dislocation of lens
    - severe myopia
- increased risk of arterial and venous thromboembolism
- also malar flush, livedo reticularis

Investigations  

- increased homocysteine levels in serum and urine
- cyanide-nitroprusside test: also positive in cystinuria

Treatment is vitamin B6 (pyridoxine) supplements. 
- Cystathionine beta synthase requires vitamin B6 as a co factor
- In homocystinuria more co factor is needed to convert homocysteine to cystathionine

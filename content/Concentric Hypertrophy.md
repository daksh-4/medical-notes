# Concentric Hypertrophy

Concentric hypertrophy
- Pressure overload chronically
- Sarcomeres added in parallel 
- Left ventricular mass increased 
- Wall thickness increased
- ![[Pasted image 20241102121109.png|500]]
- Hypertension and aortic stenosis 
- Both raise pressure in LV cavity 
- Decreased compliance due to thickness
- Often seen in diastolic heart failure

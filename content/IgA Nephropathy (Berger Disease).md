# IgA Nephropathy (Berger Disease)

1. IgA immune complex deposition in mesangium of glomeruli; most common
    
    nephropathy worldwide
    
2. Presents during childhood as episodic gross or microscopic hematuria with RBC
    
    casts, usually following mucosal infections (e.g., gastroenteritis)

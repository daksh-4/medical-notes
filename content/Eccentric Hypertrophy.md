# Eccentric Hypertrophy

Eccentric hypertrophy 
- Longer myocytes because more sarcomeres added in series

- Left ventricular mass increased 
- Wall thickness is not increased
- Classically occurs in volume overload so AR or MR and cardiomyopathy

# Gastroschisis
  
Gastroschisis describes a congenital defect in the anterior abdominal wall just lateral to the umbilical cord. Not covered by peritoneum
  
Management  

- vaginal delivery may be attempted
- newborns should go to theatre as soon as possible after delivery, e.g. within 4 hours

Associations

- Socioeconomic deprivation - maternal age <20, maternal alcohol/tobacco use

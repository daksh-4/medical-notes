# Lichen Simplex Chronicus

1. Characterized by hyperplasia of the vulvar squamous epithelium
    
2. Presents as leukoplakia with thick, leathery vulvar skin
    
3. Associated with chronic irritation and scratching
    
4. Benign; no increased risk of squamous cell carcinoma

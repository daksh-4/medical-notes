# Leiomyosarcoma

1. Malignant proliferation of smooth muscle arising from the myometrium
    
2. Arises de novo; leiomyosarcomas do not arise from leiomyomas.
    
3. Usually seen in postmenopausal women
    
4. Gross exam often shows a single lesion with areas of necrosis and hemorrhage;
    
    histological features include necrosis, mitotic activity, and cellular atypia.

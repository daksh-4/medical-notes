# Vasculitis

Intima - composed of endothelial cells on a basement membrane
Media - smooth muscle
Adventitia - connective tissue

Clinical features
- Nonspecific symptoms - fever, fatigue, weight loss, myalgia
- Symptoms of organ ischaemia 
	- Endothelial cells get damaged, exposing sub endothelial collagen, leading to formation of a thrombus
	- Fibrosis of the blood vessel wall after inflammation also narrows the lumen 

Large Vessel
- Involves the aorta or its branches

Medium vessel
- Involves muscular arteries like the renal artery

Small vessel
- Arterioles, capillaries and venules

# Lymphogranuloma Venereum

IV. LYMPHOGRANULOMA VENEREUM

1. Necrotizing granulomatous inflammation of the inguinal lymphatics and lymph
    
    nodes
    
2. Sexually transmitted disease caused by Chlamydia trachomatis (serotypes Ll-L3). Chlamydia can't make its own ATP so it grows inside cells. 
    
3. Eventually heals with fibrosis; perianal involvement may result in rectal stricture.

# Prinzmental Angina

Vasospasm of the coronary artery

Episodic chest pain that is unrelated to exertion

Represents reversible injury to the myocytes. 

Show ST elevation. Coronary artery is in the epicardium and if you cut off the blood supply completely, there is transmural ischaemia

Nitrates or calcium channel blockers

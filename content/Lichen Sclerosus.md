# Lichen Sclerosus

1. Characterized by thinning of the epidermis and fibrosis (sclerosis) of the dermis
    
2. Presents as a white patch (leukoplakia) with parchment-like vulvar skin
    
3. Most commonly seen in postmenopausal women; possible autoimmune etiology
    
4. Benign, but associated with a slightly increased risk for squamous cell carcinoma

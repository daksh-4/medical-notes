# Myelofibrosis

Neoplastic proliferation of mature myeloid cels, especially megakaryocytes

Associated with JAK2 kinase mutation

Megakaryocytes make excess platelet derived growth factor (PDGF) 
This results in marrow fibrosis 

This shows too much pink collagen around the bone

![[Pasted image 20240912115313.png]]

Clinical features
- Splenomegaly due to extramedullary haematopoiesis (body goes back to embryological way of making blood in the spleen)
- Leukoerythroblastic smear 
	- When blood cells are made in the bone marrow only small cells are allowed to pass through the reticulin gates
	- But this doesn't exist in the spleen
	- This means that immature blood cells can leak into the blood as well as nucleated RBCs.
- Increased risk of infection, thrombosis and bleeding
	- Because the spleen can't make that many blood cells 

Tear drop cells - red blood cells get squeezed out of the fibrotic bone marrow

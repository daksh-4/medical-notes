# Periductal Mastitis

Epithelium of the lactiferous duct has specialised epithelium which requires lots of vitamin A to maintain. 

Inflammation of the subareolar ducts

Usually seen in smokers

Relative vitamin A deficiency results in squamous metaplasia of lactiferous ducts, producing duct blockage due to keratin and inflammation. 

When you block a tube you get inflammation behind it. Fibrosis due to inflammatory response behind the nipple contain myofibroblasts which contract and pull the nipple back 

C. Clinically presents as a subareolar mass with nipple retraction

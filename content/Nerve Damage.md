# Nerve Damage

Peripheral nerve damage - normally due to trauma

Mild - Neurapraxia
Moderate - Axonotmesis 
Severe Neurotmesis

Can result in weakness or sensory loss

![[Pasted image 20241106134736.png]]

UMN - cortex, internal capsule, corticospinal tract

LMN - Brain stem, spinal cord (anterior cord)

Upper motor damage (pyramidal signs)
- ﻿﻿Spastic paralysis (stiff, rigid muscles)
- ﻿﻿Hyperreflexia
- ﻿﻿Muscle overactive
- ﻿﻿Clasp knife spasticity: passive movement → initial resistance, sudden release

Lower motor neurone damage
- Flaccid paralysis
- Fasciculations
- Loss of reflexes

UMN cross just below the medulla 

![[Pasted image 20241106135749.png|500]]

Bulbar muscles are supplied by [[Cranial Nerves]] in the brain stem
- V jaw
- VII face
- IX swallowing
- X palate
- XI head
- XII tongue

Bulbar palsy
- Cranial nerve damage 
- LMN 
- Absent jaw/gag reflex
- Flaccid/wasted tongue

Pseudobulbar
- Corticobulbar damage
- UMN signs
- Exaggerated gag reflex
- Spastic tongue 
- Spastic dysarthria - slow, laboured and harsh speech

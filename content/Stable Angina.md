# Stable Angina

Atherosclerosis of the coronary artery with >70% stenosis, reversible injury to the myocytes in the myocardium (cellular swelling). 

Presentation
- Chest pain that lasts less than 20 minutes that radiates to the left arm or jaw
- Sweating (diaphoresis)
- Shortness of breath

ECG shows ST depression due to sub endocardial ischaemia. Relieved by rest or nitroglycerin

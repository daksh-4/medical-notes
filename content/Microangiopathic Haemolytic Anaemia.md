# Microangiopathic Haemolytic Anaemia

Pathologic formation of platelet microthrombi in small vessels. As the red blood cell passes over the platelet microthrombi, there is shearing of the blood cell (becoming a schistocyte). This results in haemolysis. 

![[Pasted image 20240904101009.png]]

Platelets are consumed in the formation of microthrombi. Schistocyte in the middle also called helmet cells

Seen in TTP, HUS, HELLP, DIC, prosthetic heart valves, aortic stenosis

TTP 

Patients get microthrombi due to a decrease in the protein ADAMSTS13. 
- Normally when you make vWf, it is made as a multimer which are degraded over time and chopped into monomers. 
	- This is done by ADAMSTS13. 
	- Large multimers of vWf can result in platelet adhesion

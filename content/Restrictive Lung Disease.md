# Restrictive Lung Disease

Problem with filling the lung

Decreased total lung capacity
Very Decreased FVC and slightly decreased FEV1
Ratio is increased >0.7

Lots of fibrosis which increases the elastic recoil of the lung which allows you to squeeze air out in the first one second and there is no collapse of the airways. 

Most commonly due to interstitial disease of the lung - thickening of lung tissue by fibrosis. May also arise with chest wall abnormalities like obesity.

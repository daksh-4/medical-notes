# Autoimmune Lymphoproliferative Syndrome

- Autoimmune lymphoproliferative syndrome (ALPS) - mutations in the fas apoptosis pathway.
    
    - Cells form IgG against cells in the blood so can get anaemia. Enlargement of the lymph nodes and hepatosplenomegaly as that is where lymphoid cells lie. Because of rapid divisions of lymphoid cells they can get lymphoma too.

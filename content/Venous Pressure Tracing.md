# Venous Pressure Tracing

![[Pasted image 20241102182823.png|500]]

The tricuspid closes at the c wave and opens at the peak of the v wave

Large a wave - tricuspid stenosis 

Cannon a wave - AV dissociation

Absent a wave - atrial fibrillation due to no organised atrial contraction

Giant V waves - tricuspid regurgitation

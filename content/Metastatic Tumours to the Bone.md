# Metastatic Tumours to the Bone

1. More common than primary tumors
    
2. Usually result in osteolytic (punched-out) lesions

Prostatic carcinoma classically produces osteoblastic lesions. Activation of the osteoblast leads in laying down bone next to the tumour leading to sclerosis

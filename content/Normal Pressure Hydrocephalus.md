# Normal Pressure Hydrocephalus

Increased CSF resulting in dilated ventricles

Can cause dementia in adults; usually idiopathic

Leads to stretching of the corona radiata which leads to:

Presents as triad of urinary incontinence, gait instability, and dementia ("wet, wobbly, and wacky")

Lumbar puncture improves symptoms; treatment is ventriculoperitoneal shunting.

Normally CSF is drained by arachnoid granulations which provide access to the venous sinuses. In NPH, there is decreased absorption into the arachnoid granulations.

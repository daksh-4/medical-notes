# Oral Herpes

Vesicles involving the oral mucosa that rupture resulting in a shallow painful red ulcers

Usually due to HSV 1

Primary infection occurs in childhood
- Lesions heal but the virus remains dormant in the ganglia of the trigeminal nerve
- Stress and sunlight cause reactivation of the virus
- Usually on the lip and arise as a cold sore

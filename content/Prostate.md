# Prostate

1. Small, round organ that lies at the base of the bladder encircling the urethra
    
2. Sits anterior to the rectum; posterior aspect of prostate is palpable by digital rectal exam (DRE).
    
3. Consists of glands and stroma (Fig. 14.9)
    
    1. Glands are composed of an inner layer of luminal cells and an outer layer of basal cells; secrete alkaline, milky fluid that is added to sperm and seminal vesicle fluid to make semen.
        
    2. Glands and stroma are maintained by androgens.

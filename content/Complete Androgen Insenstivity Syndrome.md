# Complete Androgen Insenstivity Syndrome

- ﻿﻿Mutation of androgen receptor in males (XY)
- ﻿﻿No ovaries; testes form in utero (SRY gene)
- ﻿﻿No cellular response to androgens
- ﻿﻿No internal or external male genital development

Sertoli cells (testes) present → MIH
- ﻿﻿Degeneration of mullerian structures
- ﻿﻿Absent uterus, fallopian tubes

![[Pasted image 20250408122714.png]]

- ﻿﻿At puberty:
	- ﻿﻿Breasts develop (testosterone → estrogen)
	- ﻿﻿No armpit/pubic hair (depends on androgens)
- ﻿﻿Amenorrhea (no uterus)
- ﻿﻿Abdominal testes

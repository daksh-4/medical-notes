# Craniopharyngioma

Tumour that arises from epithelial remnants of Rathke's pouch

Presents as a supratentorial mass in a child or young adult; may compress the optic chiasm leading to bitemporal hemianopia

Calcifications are commonly seen on imaging (derived from "tooth-like" tissue from the floor of the mouth).

Benign, but tends to recur after resection

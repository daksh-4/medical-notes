# Upper Extremity Nerve Injuries

ARM-U

NAMe SOME

Axillary nerve C5-C6 - Neck fracture (surgical) - Can't abduct

Radial Nerve C5-T1 - Axilla or Midshaft fracture - Wrist Drop

Median Nerve C5-T1 - Supracondylar fracture (also ==brachial artery==) - Ape hand deformity 
- Brachial artery injury would present with cold fingers and pain in the forearm on passive extension of the fingers

Ulnar Nerve C8-T1 - Outstretched Fall (injuring the hook of hamate), Medial Epicondyle fracture - Claw hand deformity

# Renal Artery Stenosis

Renal artery stenosis - increased plasma renin - angiotensin constricts efferent arteriole increasing peripheral resistance and aldosterone causes reabsorption of sodium which increases plasma volume. 

Atherosclerosis 
Fibromuscular dysplasia 

![[Pasted image 20241208170100.png]]

One kidney is sensing low blood pressure and the other kidney is sensing high blood pressure.

Normal kidney can't compensate fully of the increased renin being secreted by the stenotic kidney, leading to high blood pressure. 

In the setting of RAS - normal GFR depends on angiotensin II
- A 2 leads to efferent arteriole vasoconstriction 
- which maintains the GFR
ACE inhibitors can precipitate renal failure

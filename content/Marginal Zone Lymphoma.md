# Marginal Zone Lymphoma

Neoplastic small B cells (CD20+) that expand marginal zone. Zone that is outside the mantle. 

Associated with chronic inflammatory states
Most lymph nodes don't have a marginal zone, but it becomes present by activation in the germinal centre. (Post-germinal centre B cells)

Hashimoto's thyroiditis, Sjorgren syndrome, H pylori gastritis. 

H pylori gastritis - Marginal zone lymphoma in mucosal sites (e.g. stomach). Gastric MALToma may regress with regression of the disease

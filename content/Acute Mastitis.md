# Acute Mastitis

1. Bacterial infection ofthe breast, usually due to Staphylococcus aureus
    
2. Associated with breast-feeding; fissures develop in the nipple providing a route of entry for microbes.
    
3. Presents as an erythematous breast with purulent nipple discharge; may progress to abscess formation

D. Treatment involves continued drainage (e.g., feeding) and antibiotics (e.g.,

flucloxacillin). And warm compresses

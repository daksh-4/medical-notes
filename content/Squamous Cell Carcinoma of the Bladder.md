# Squamous Cell Carcinoma of the Bladder

1. Malignant proliferation of squamous cells, usually involving the bladder
    
2. Arises in a background of squamous metaplasia (normal bladder surface is not lined by squamous epithelium)
    
3. Risk factors include chronic cystitis (older woman), Schistosoma hematobium infection (Egyptian male), and long-standing nephrolithiasis.

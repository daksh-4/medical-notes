# Hypersensitivity Types

| Type                                   | Mechanism                                    | Examples                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Type I - Anaphylactic**              | Antigen reacts with IgE bound to mast cells  | • Anaphylaxis  <br>• Atopy (e.g. asthma, eczema and hayfever)                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Type II - Cell bound**               | IgG or IgM binds to antigen on cell surface  | • Autoimmune haemolytic anaemia  <br>• ITP  <br>• Goodpasture's syndrome  <br>• Pernicious anaemia  <br>• Acute haemolytic transfusion reactions  <br>• Rheumatic fever  <br>• Pemphigus vulgaris / bullous pemphigoid                                |
| **Type III - Immune complex**          | Free antigen and antibody (IgG, IgA) combine | • Serum sickness  <br>• Systemic lupus erythematosus  <br>• Post-streptococcal glomerulonephritis  <br>• Extrinsic allergic alveolitis (especially acute phase)                                                                                       |
| **Type IV - Delayed hypersensitivity** | T-cell mediated                              | • Tuberculosis / tuberculin skin reaction  <br>• Graft versus host disease  <br>• Allergic contact dermatitis  <br>• Scabies  <br>• Extrinsic allergic alveolitis (especially chronic phase)  <br>• Multiple sclerosis  <br>• Guillain-Barre syndrome |

In recent times a further category has been added:  

| Type       | Mechanism                                                                                                                             | Examples                                   |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| **Type V** | Antibodies that recognise and bind to the cell surface receptors.   <br>  <br>This either stimulating them or blocking ligand binding | • Graves' disease  <br>• Myasthenia gravis |

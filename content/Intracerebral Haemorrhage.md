# Intracerebral Haemorrhage

A. Bleeding into brain parenchyma  

B. Classically due to rupture of Charcot-Bouchard microaneurysms of the lenticulostriate vessels in the [[Basal Ganglia]] (most common site).

Complication of hypertension; treatment of hypertension reduces incidence by half.

[[Basal Ganglia]] is the most common site (Fig. 17.7).

Presents as severe headache, nausea, vomiting, and eventual coma

# Paroxysmal Supraventricular Tachycardia

Sudden onset/offset 
- Contrast with sinus tachy

Electrical activity originates above the ventricle in contrast with v tach so there are narrow QRS complexes

Often causes sudden onset palpitations

Most common cause is AVNRT [[AVNRT]]

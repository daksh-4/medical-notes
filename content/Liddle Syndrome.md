# Liddle Syndrome

- ﻿﻿Genetic disorder
- ﻿﻿Increased activity of ENaC
- ﻿﻿Similar clinical syndrome to hyperaldosteronism
	- ﻿﻿Hypertension
	- ﻿﻿Hypokalemia
- ﻿﻿Aldosterone levels low

![[Screenshot 2024-12-08 at 16.41.56.png]]

Spironolactone doesn't work in this condition, use amiloride instead because it directly inhibits the ENaC channel

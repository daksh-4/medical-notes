# Mycosis fungoides

Neoplastic proliferation of mature CD4+ T cells

Cells infiltrate skin, producing rash, plaques or nodules

Aggregates of neoplastic T-cells in epidermis are called Pautrier microabscesses. 

Cells can spread to involve blood 
- Known as sezary syndrome
- Characteristic lymphocytes with cerebriform nuclei are seen on blood smear

# Vertigo

Vertigo may be defined as the false sensation that the body or environment is moving.  
  
The table below lists the main characteristics of the most important causes of vertigo  

|**Disorder**|**Notes**|
|---|---|
|**Viral labyrinthitis**|Recent viral infection  <br>Sudden onset  <br>Nausea and vomiting  <br>Hearing may be affected|
|**Vestibular neuronitis**|Recent viral infection  <br>Recurrent vertigo attacks lasting hours or days  <br>No hearing loss|
|**Benign paroxysmal positional vertigo**|Gradual onset  <br>Triggered by change in head position  <br>Each episode lasts 10-20 seconds|
|**Meniere's disease**|Associated with hearing loss, tinnitus and sensation of fullness or pressure in one or both ears|
|**Vertebrobasilar ischaemia**|Elderly patient  <br>Dizziness on extension of neck|
|**Acoustic neuroma**|Hearing loss, vertigo, tinnitus  <br>Absent corneal reflex is important sign  <br>Associated with neurofibromatosis type 2|

Other causes of vertigo include  

- posterior circulation stroke
- trauma
- multiple sclerosis
- ototoxicity e.g. gentamicin

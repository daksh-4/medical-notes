# Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation

Pathological activation of the coagulation cascade leading to widespread microthrombi resulting in ischaemia and infarction. 

Consumption of platelets and factors results in bleeding, especially from IV sites and mucosal surfaces. 

Almost always secondary to another disease process
- Obstetric complication - amniotic fluid contains tissue thromboplastin which activates the coagulation cascade. 
- Sepsis - endotoxins and cytokines (IL-1 and TNF) can activate the coagulation cascade
- Adenocarcinoma - mucin
- Acute promyelocytic leukemia - cells are loaded with primary granules and form Auer rods. If granules enter circulation, activates coagulation cascade
- Rattlesnake bite - venom

Lab findings
- Low platelet count
- Increased PT/PTT
- DECREASED FIBRINOGEN
- Microangiopathic haemolytic anaemia 
- Elevated fibrin split produces (D-dimer)

Treatment
- Address the underlying cause
- Transfuse blood products and cryoprecipitate

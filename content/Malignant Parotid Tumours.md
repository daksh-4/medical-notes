# Malignant Parotid Tumours

| **Tumour type**          | **Features**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mucoepidermoid carcinoma | - 30% of all parotid malignancies<br>- Usually low potential for local invasiveness and metastasis (depends mainly on grade)                                                                                                                    |
| Adenoid cystic carcinoma | - Unpredictable growth pattern<br>- Tendency for perineural spread<br>- Nerve growth may display skip lesions resulting in incomplete excision<br>- Distant metastasis more common (visceral rather than nodal spread)<br>- 5 year survival 35% |
| Mixed tumours            | - Often a malignancy occurring in a previously benign parotid lesion                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Acinic cell carcinoma    | - Intermediate grade malignancy<br>- May show perineural invasion<br>- Low potential for distant metastasis<br>- 5 year survival 80%                                                                                                            |
| Adenocarcinoma           | - Develops from secretory portion of gland<br>- Risk of regional nodal and distant metastasis<br>- 5 year survival depends upon stage at presentation, may be up to 75% with small lesions with no nodal involvement                            |
| Lymphoma                 | - Large rubbery lesion, may occur in association with Warthins tumours<br>- Diagnosis should be based on regional nodal biopsy rather than parotid resection<br>- Treatment is with chemotherapy (and radiotherapy)                             |

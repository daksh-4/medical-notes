# Asthma

Often associated with allergic rhinitis, eczema and a family history of atopy

Pathogenesis
- Allergens induce TH2 phenotype in CD4+ T cells of genetically susceptible individuals 
- Th2 cells secrete IL-4, IL-5, IL-10
	- IL-4 - allows the plasma cell to class switch to IgE
	- IL-5 calls in eosinophils
	- IL-10 inhibits the production Th1 and promotes Th2 production

Reexposure to allergen
- Results in IgE mediated activation of mast cells and dumps histamine granules leads to vasodilation (arterioles) and increased vascular permeability (post capillary venules). 
- Mast cells then perpetuate inflammation by making leukotrienes C4 D4 and E4. This causes vasoconstriction and increased vascular permeability by contracting pericytes and cause bronchoconstriction. 
- Inflammation perpetuates bronchoconstriction  
  
Clinical features (episodic)
- Dyspnoea and wheezing
- Productive cough; Curschmann spirals admixed with Charcot-Leyden crystals
- ![[Pasted image 20240925144000.png]] Charcot Leyden crystals 
- Severe unrelenting attack can result in status asthmaticus and death. 

Nonallergic causes of asthma
- Exercise 
- Viral infection
- Aspirin
- Occupational exposure

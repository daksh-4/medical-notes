# Cellulitis

1. Deeper (dermal and subcutaneous) infection, usually due to S aureus or S pyogenes
    
2. Presents as a red, tender, swollen rash with fever
    
3. Risk factors include recent surgery, trauma, or insect bite.
    
4. Can progress to necrotizing fasciitis with necrosis of subcutaneous tissues due to
    
    infection with anaerobic 'flesh-eating' bacteria

l. Production of CO, leads to crepitus. 2. Surgical emergency

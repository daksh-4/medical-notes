# Still's disease

AKA systemic onset juvenile idiopathic arthritis

- pyrexia
- salmon-pink rash
- lymphadenopathy
- arthritis
- uveitis
- anorexia and weight loss

Investigations  

- ANA may be positive, especially in oligoarticular JIA
- rheumatoid factor is usually negative

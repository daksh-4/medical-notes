# Anaemias due to underproduction

Causes of microcytic and [[Macrocytic Anaemia]]

Renal failure - low EPO
Damage to bone marrow precursor cells 

LOW RETICULOCYTE COUNT

Parvovirus B19 infects progenitor red cells
- Temporarily halts erythropoiesis
- Leads to significant anaemia in the setting of preexisting stress
- Supportive treatment

Aplastic anaemia
- damage to haematopoietic stem cells
- Results in pancytopenia with low reticulocyte count
- Drugs or chemicals ()\
- Viral infections
- Autoimmune damage
- Classic finding is empty marrow with no HSPs
- Treatment
	- Cessation of causative drugs
	- Transfusions
	- Marrow stimulating factors (EPO, GM-CSF granulocytic precursor driver, G-CSF)
	- Immunosuppression 
	- BMT (last resort) bone marrow transplant

Myelophthisic process
- Pathological process that replaces bone marrow like cancer
- Haematopoiesis is impaired resulting in pancytopenia

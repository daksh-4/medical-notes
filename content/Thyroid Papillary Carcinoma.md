# Thyroid Papillary Carcinoma

1. Most common type of thyroid carcinoma (SO% of cases)
    
2. Exposure to ionizing radiation in childhood is a major risk factor.
    
3. Comprised of papillae lined by cells with clear, 'Orphan Annie eye' nuclei and
    
    nuclear grooves (Fig. 15.5A); papillae are often associated with psammoma bodies
    
    (Fig. 15.5B).
    
4. Often spreads to cervical (neck) lymph nodes, but prognosis is excellent (10-year
    
    survival> 95%)

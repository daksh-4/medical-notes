# Pyloric Stenosis

Congenital hypertrophy of pyloric smooth muscle; more common in males

Classically presents two weeks after birth
- It takes time for the stenosis to develop
- Pressure build up in the pyloric sphincter 

Features
- Projectile non-bilious vomiting
	- Contents of the stomach have not met the bowel as this happens after the pyloric sphincter
- Visible peristalsis due to high pressure
- Olive-like mass in the abdomen due to hypertrophy

Treatment is myotomy to cut away the muscle

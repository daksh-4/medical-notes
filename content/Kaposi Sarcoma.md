# Kaposi Sarcoma

Low grade malignant proliferation of endothelial cells

Associated with HHV-8

Presents as
- Purple patches, plaques or nodules on the skin
	- They don't blanche when presssed because there is no vasculature just endothelial cells
- May involve visceral organs

Seen in 
- Older Eastern European males
	- Skin, surgical removal
- AIDS
	- Can spread early and go into other tissues. Treat with antiretrovirals
- Transplant recipients 
	- Give descreased immunosuppression to fight the HHV-8

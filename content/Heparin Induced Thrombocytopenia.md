# Heparin Induced Thrombocytopenia

Heparin forms a complex with platelet factor 4 and IgG antibodies form against it. 

This results in consumption of the platelets from the spleen. 

One of the complications of this is that fragments of destroyed platelets are pumped around the body and activate the remaining platelets, leading to thrombosis. 

Stop the heparin and give another anticoagulant but don't give warfarin (as there is an increased risk of warfarin skin necrosis.)

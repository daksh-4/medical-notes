# Polycythaemia Vera

Neoplastic proliferation of mature myeloid cells especially RBCs

Granulocytes and platelets are also increased 

Associated with the JAK2 kinase mutation

Symptoms
- Hyperviscosity
- Blurry vision and headaches 
- Increased risk of venous thrombosis (hepatic vein thrombosis Budd Chiari syndrome leading to infection of the liver)
- Flushed face due to congestion
- Itching after bathing. 
	- Along with RBCs they also have increased mast cells. Bathing sets off the mast cells leading to itching

Treatment
- Phlebotomy
	- Without treatment death usually occurs within one year
- Second line treatment is hydroxyurea

PV must be distinguished from reactive polycythaemia (like in COPD leading to hypoxia which leads to more EPO and more red blood cells or tumour that makes EPO)
- In PV, SaO2 is normal and EPO is decreased
- Opposite in lung disease
- In ectopic EPO production, EPO is high and SaO2 is normal. The tumour that causes this is renal cell carcinoma

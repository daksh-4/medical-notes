# Cardiac Physiology

Pulse pressure = systolic - diastolic
It is proportional to the stroke volume

4 determinants of cardiac output
- Preload
	- Add volume, slow heart rate (more filling time), constrict veins 
		- Alpha 1 receptors respond to sympathetic stimulation to constrict
- Afterload
- Contractility
- Heart rate

Why is there a split S2?
# ASD

Defect in the septum that divides left and right atria. 

Most common type is ostium secundum

Ostium primum type is associated with Down's syndrome 

Results in a left to right shunt
- Split S2 on auscultation - extra volume in the right atrium results in delayed closure of the pulmonic valve, resulting in splitting. 
- Paradoxical emboli are important complication

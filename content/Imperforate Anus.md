[[Renal Agenesis]]

# Imperforate Anus

Hindgut and ectoderm meet to form anus and abscence of anal opening = imperforate anus

Commonly associated with genitourinary malformations such as renal agenesis and bladder exstrophy

Presentations
- Failure to pass meconium
- Meconium from urethra or vagina due to fistula

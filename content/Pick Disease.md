# Pick Disease

Degenerative disease of the frontal and temporal cortex; spares the parietal and occipital lobes

Characterized by round aggregates of tau protein (Pick bodies) in neurons of the cortex. (as opposed to tangles in Alzheimer's )

Behavioural and language symptoms arise early; eventually progresses to dementia

# Regulation of Blood Pressure

Baroreceptors
- ﻿﻿Blood pressure sensors via stretch
- Signals go to the nucleus solitarius
- ﻿﻿Response via autonomic nervous system
	- ﻿﻿Sympathetic and parasympathetic
- ﻿﻿Modify:
	- ﻿﻿Heart rate/contractility
	- ﻿﻿Arterial tone (vasoconstriction)
	- ﻿﻿Venous tone (more tone = more preload to ventricle)
	- ﻿﻿Renal renin release (RAAS) 
- Aortic arch 
	- Senses elevated blood pressure but bad at sensing low bp
	- Vagus nerve
- Carotid sinus
	- Most important can sense high and low
	- Glossopharyngeal nerve IX

Increased capillary pressure seen in heart failure

Decreased plasma proteins seen in nephrotic syndrome and liver failure

Increased capillary permeability seen in toxins, infections and burns 

Increased interstitial osmotic pressure seen in lymphatic blockage

Intracellular fluid - 1st space
- ﻿﻿About 2/3 body fluid

Extracellular fluid - 2nd space
- ﻿﻿About 1/3 body fluid

Third spacing - fluid where it should NOT be

- ﻿﻿Pleural effusions
- ﻿﻿Ascites
- ﻿﻿Cerebral edema
- ﻿﻿Low intravascular volume/High total volume

Occurs post-op, sepsis

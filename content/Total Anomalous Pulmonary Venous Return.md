# Total Anomalous Pulmonary Venous Return

Pulmonary veins drain to the venous system instead of the left side of the heart. Lungs drain to:
- Innominate brachiocephalic veins connecting to the SVC
- Coronary sinus
- Postal vein

Right atrium and right ventricle dilate due to increased blood flow into these regions

Must have a right to left shunt
- ASD
- PDA

Mixed blood to the body leading to low O2 sats. 

Early cyanosis in a baby

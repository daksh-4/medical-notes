# Lymphadenopathy

Painful LAD is seen with acute infection
Painless LAD is seen with chronic inflammation, metastatic carcinoma or lymphoma

In inflammation, enlargement is due to hyperplasia of lymph node regions

Follicles - rheumatoid arthritis and early HIV because lymph node cells are also CD4+ 
Paracortex - viral infection
Sinus histiocytes - lymph nodes draining tissues with cancer

![[Pasted image 20240912120128.png]]
Regions of the lymph node

Lymphoma - neoplastic proliferation of lymphoid cells that forms in mass can be in the node or somewhere else

Divided into non-Hodgkins and Hodgkins.

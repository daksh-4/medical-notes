# Acute Leukaemia

Neoplastic proliferation of blasts - defined as the accumulation of >20% blasts in the bone marrow

Blasts crowd out normal haematopoiesis - results in acute presentation with anaemia, thrombocytopenia or neutropenia. 

Blasts enter the blood resulting in a high WBC. 

Blasts are large immature cells (little cytoplasm), often with punched out nucleoli on blood smear

It is hard to tell if it's myeloid or lymphoid. 

Lymphoblasts are tDt+ 
Myeloblasts are myeloperoxidase+. Myeloperoxidase may condense into an Auer rod.

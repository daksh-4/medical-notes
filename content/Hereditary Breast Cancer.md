# Hereditary Breast Cancer

Represents 10% of breast cancer cases

Clinical features that suggest hereditary breast cancer include multiple first-degree relatives with breast cancer, tumor at an early age (premenopausal), and multiple tumors in a single patient.

BRCAJ and BRCA2 mutations are the most important single gene mutations associated with hereditary breast cancer.

I. BRCAl mutation is associated with breast and ovarian carcinoma (and in the fallopian tube). 2. BRCA2 mutation is associated with breast carcinoma in males.

D. Women with a genetic propensity to develop breast cancer may choose to undergo removal of both breasts (bilateral mastectomy) to decrease the risk of developing carcinoma.  

A small risk for cancer remains because breast tissue sometimes extends into the axilla or subcutaneous tissue of the chest wall.

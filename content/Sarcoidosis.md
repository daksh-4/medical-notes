# Sarcoidosis

Systemic disease characterised by noncaseating granulomas in multiple organs.  

Classically seen in African American females

Unknown cause; likely due to CD4+ helper T cell response to an unknown antigen

![[Pasted image 20240925153353.png]] asteroid body

Granulomas most commonly involve the hilar lymph nodes and lung 

Leading to [[Restrictive Lung Disease]]

Other involved tissues
- Uveitis
- Cutaneous nodules or erythema nodosum
- Salivary and lacrimal glands (mimics Sjögren's syndrome)
- Almost any tissue can be involved

Clinical features
- Cough and SOB
- Elevated serum ACE
- Hypercalcaemia - granulomas have 1 alpha hydroxylase activity and can activate vitamin D. Same goes for any other condition with non caseating granulomas (like berylliosis )
- Treatment is steroids often resolves spontaneously without treatment.

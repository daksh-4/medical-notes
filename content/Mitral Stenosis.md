# Mitral Stenosis

Narrowing of the mitral valve orifice
Usually due to chronic rheumatic valve disease

Opening snap followed by diastolic rumble
Loudest in left lateral decubitus position on expiration

Volume overload leads to dilatation of the left atrium
- Pulmonary congestion - link to haemosiderin leaden macrophages
- Pulmonary hypertension
- Atrial fibrillation - stretching of the conduction system
- Malar flush

# Churg Strauss Syndrome

Small vessel

Necrotising granulomatous [[Vasculitis]] with eosinophils

Involves multiple organs especially lungs and heart 
Asthma and peripheral eosinophilia
p-ANCA levels correlate with disease activity

Granulomas, asthma and eosinophila to distinguish with GPA.

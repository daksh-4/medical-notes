# Polycystic Kidney Disease

Inherited defect leading to bilateral enlarged kidneys with cysts in the renal cortex and medulla (Fig. 12.4)

Autosomal recessive form presents in infants as worsening renal failure and hypertension; newborns may present with Potter sequence (the flattening of the face and extremities due to oligohydramnios).

Associated with congenital hepatic fibrosis (leads to portal hypertension) and hepatic cysts  

-----

Autosomal dominant form presents in young adults as hypertension (due to increased renin), hematuria, and worsening renal failure.

Due to mutation in the APKDl or APKD2 gene; cysts develop over time.

Associated with berry aneurysm, hepatic cysts, and mitral valve prolapse. Cysts in the brain (cystic dilatation), liver and kidneys.

# Adult T Cell Leukaemia Lymphoma

Neoplastic proliferation of mature CD4+ T cells

Associated with HTLV-1 (Japanese and Caribbean) human T cell leukaemia virus 1

Clinical features
- Rash
	 - Lots of T cell leukaemias go to the skin
- Generalised lymphadenopathy with hepatosplenomegaly
- Lytic bone lesions with hypercalcaemia. (HIGH YIELD)

# Bowen's Disease

Bowen's disease is a type of precancerous dermatosis that is a precursor to squamous cell carcinoma as a squamous cell carcinoma in situ. It is more common in elderly patients. There is around a 5-10% chance of developing invasive skin cancer if left untreated.  
  
Features  

- red, scaly patches
    - often 10-15 mm in size
    - slow-growing
    - often occur on sun-exposed areas such as the head (e.g. temples) and neck, lower limbs

Management options:  

- may sometimes be diagnosed and managed in primary care if clear diagnosis or repeat episode
- topical 5-fluorouracil
    - typically used twice daily for 4 weeks
    - often results in significant inflammation/erythema. Topical steroids are often given to control this
- cryotherapy
- excision

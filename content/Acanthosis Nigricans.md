# Acanthosis Nigricans

A. Epidermal hyperplasia with darkening of the skin ('velvet-like' skin, Fig. 19.9); often involves the axilla or groin

Hyperkeratosis

B. Associated with insulin resistance (e.g., non-insulin-dependent diabetes) or malignancy (especially gastric carcinoma)

Causes  

- type 2 [[Diabetes Mellitus]]
- gastrointestinal cancer
- obesity
- polycystic ovarian syndrome
- acromegaly
- Cushing's disease
- [[Hypothyroidism]]
- familial
- Prader-Willi syndrome
- drugs
    - combined oral contraceptive pill
    - nicotinic acid

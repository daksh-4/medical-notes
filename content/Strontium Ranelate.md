[[Osteoporosis]]

![[Pasted image 20250122124457.png]]

1. PTH binds to the PTH1R receptors on osteoblasts
2. Osteoblasts produce RANKL and sRANKL
3. RANKL binds to RANK in preosteoclasts to induce prliferation and maturation of osteoclasts
4. RANKL binds to RANK in osteoclasts membrane leading to activation and bone resorption 
5. Osteoblasts produce OPG, a decoy for RANKL preventing interaction with RANK and leading to bone formation

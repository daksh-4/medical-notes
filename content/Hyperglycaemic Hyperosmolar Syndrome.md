# Hyperglycaemic Hyperosmolar Syndrome

High glucose leading to diuresis 

Severe hydration

Different from DKA
- Few or no ketone bodies because INSULIN IS PRESENT
- Usually no acidosis
- Very high serum osmolarity which may lead to CNS dysfunction

Symptoms
- Polyuria, polydipsia
- Dehydration
- Mental status changes - confusion and coma

Treatment is similar to DKA (insulin and IV fluids)

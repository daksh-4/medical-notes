# Soft Tissue Tumours

I. LIPOMA

1. Benign tumor of adipose tissue
    
2. Most common benign soft tissue tumor in adults

II. LIPOSARCOMA

1. Malignant tumor of adipose tissue
    
2. Most common malignant soft tissue tumor in adults
    
3. Lipoblast is the characteristic cell.

III. RHABDOMYOMA

1. Benign tumor of skeletal muscle
    
2. Cardiac rhabdomyoma is associated with tuberous sclerosis.

IV. RHABDOMYOSARCOMA

1. Malignant tumor of skeletal muscle
    
2. Most common malignant soft tissue tumor in children
    
3. Rhabdomyoblast is the characteristic cell; desmin positive
    
4. Most common site is the head and neck; vagina is the classic site in young girls.

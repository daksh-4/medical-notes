# VSD

Most common congenital heart defect

Associated with fetal alcohol syndrome

Left to right leads to increased pulmonary hypertension. Eventually blood will switch to right to left which results in pumping of deoxygenated blood and cyanosis. 

Eisenmenger's
- Cyanosis
- Right ventricular hypertrophy
- Polycythaemia
- Clubbing

Surgical closure for large, small ones may close spontaneously

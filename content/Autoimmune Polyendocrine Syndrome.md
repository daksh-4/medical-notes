# Autoimmune Polyendocrine Syndrome

- Autoimmune polyendocrine syndrome
    - in the thymus medulla where self tolerance is tested there are dendritic cells that present a lot of self antigens as well as medullary epithelial cell that also expresses peripheral antigens.
    - The medullary epithelial cell has a transcription factor called AIRE which is required for the expression of self antigens. If AIRE is not present, then a subset of self antigens can’t be presented resulting in autoimmune cells being released.
    - Triad of [[Hypoparathyroidism]], adrenal failure, chronic candida infections of the skin and oral mucosa.
- In the bone marrow immature B cells are tested for binding to self antigens. If binding to self antigen is present, self editing can occur: RAG genes are re-expressed to edit the light chain of the immunoglobulin so it no longer binds self antigen. Or it undergoes apoptosis.

# Volvulus

Twisting of bowel along its mesentery
Results in obstruction and disruption of the blood supply with infarction (Fig. 10.17)
Most common locations are sigmoid colon (elderly) and cecum (young adults).

|Sigmoid volvulus associations|Caecal volvulus associations|
|---|---|
|- older patients <br>- chronic constipation<br>- Chagas disease<br>- neurological conditions e.g. Parkinson's disease, Duchenne muscular dystrophy<br>- psychiatric conditions e.g. schizophrenia|- all ages<br>- adhesions<br>- pregnancy|

Features  

- constipation
- abdominal bloating
- abdominal pain
- nausea/vomiting

Investigation  

- usually diagnosed on the abdominal film
- sigmoid volvulus: large bowel obstruction (large, dilated loop of colon, often with air-fluid levels) + coffee bean sign
- caecal volvulus: small bowel obstruction may be seen

Management  

- sigmoid volvulus: rigid sigmoidoscopy with rectal tube insertion
- caecal volvulus: management is usually operative. Right hemicolectomy is often needed

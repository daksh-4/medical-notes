# Follicular Lymphoma

Summary - the translocation t(14;18) causes overexpression of BCL2. This stops death of B cells with poor antibody and affinity. More central blasts = higher grade follicular lymphoma. 

### Follicular Lymphoma

Small lymphocytes are well-differentiated and look almost normal
- They grow in the pattern of which normal lymphocytes grow. 

Neoplastic small B cells (CD20+) that make follicle-like nodules 
Clinically presents in late adulthood with painless LAD. 

Driven by t(14:18)
- Bcl2 on chromosome 18 translocates to the Ig heavy chain locus on chromosome 14. 
- This results in overexpression of Bcl2 which inhibits apoptosis
- Bcl2 stabilises the mitochondrial membrane, stopping leakage of cytochrome c to prevent apoptosis. 
- You want apoptosis to happen in the follicle of the lymph nodes because this is where they undergo somatic hypermutation and are tested against antigens. 
	- But if they fail they undergo apoptosis 
	- If there is a translocation, the faulty B cells can live

Treatment 
Reserved for patients who are symptomatic
Low dose chemotherapy or rituximab (anti CD20)

Complication
- Progression to diffuse large B cell lymphoma
- Presents as an enlarging lymph node

Distinguished from follicular hyperplasia (which can happen during infection anyway) by 
- Disruption of the normal lymph node architecture
	- There are tons of follicles throughout the lymph node
	- If it was follicular hyperplasia, follicles would only be present in the cortex
- Lack of tingible body macrophages in germinal centre
	- ![[Pasted image 20240912122129.png]]
	- If the B cells are undergoing somatic hyperplasia and dying, there are macrophages which come and clean up the dead cells. On the left you can see that the white dots which are macrophages which are eating the debris of apoptotic B cells. On the left there are no spots as the cells are not undergoing apoptosis
- Expression of Bcl2 in follicles 
- Monoclonality - a reactive proliferation would have multiclonality
	- Clonality is assessed by looking of the ratio between kappa and lamda light chain

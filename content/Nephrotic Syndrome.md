# Nephrotic Syndrome

![[Pasted image 20260829133847.png]]

A. Glomerular disorders characterized by proteinuria(> 3.5 g/day) resulting in

l. Hypoalbuminemia-pitting edema

2. Hypogammaglobulinemia-increased risk of infection
    
3. Hypercoagulable state-due to loss of antithrombin III (breaks up thrombin and stops the production of thrombin)
    
4. Hyperlipidemia and hypercholesterolemia-may result in fatty casts in urine. Blood becomes thin so the liver beefs it up a bit by making more cholesterol

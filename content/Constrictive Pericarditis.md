# Constrictive Pericarditis

- ﻿﻿Fibrous, calcified scar in pericardium
- ﻿﻿Loss of elasticity: stiff, thickened, sticky
- ﻿﻿Can result from many pericardial disease processes
	- ﻿﻿Pericarditis
	- ﻿﻿Radiation to chest
	- ﻿﻿Heart surgery

Dyspnoea

Prominent right heart failure
- Marked JVP
- Oedema
- Liver congestion which may lead to cirrhosis (nutmeg liver)

JVP is not elevated in liver failure!!!

Equalisation of pressures

Pericardial knock

Kussmaul's sign
- JVP rises with inspiration
- Right ventricle cannot accept the increased venous return
- Not seen in tamponade

# Arnold Chiari Malformation

1. Congenital extension of cerebellar tonsils through the foramen magnum

There are two types of Arnold Chiari malformation. Only one has hydrocephalus 

2. Obstruction of CSF flow can result in hydrocephalus.
    
3. May occur in association with meningomyelocele and [[Syringomyelia]]

# Mallory-Weiss Syndrome

Severe vomiting leads to a linear longitudinal laceration at the gastro-oesophageal junction. Severe vomiting in alcoholics and bulimics. 

The laceration is going to bleed and lead to haematemesis. It will also be painful 

Risk of Boerhaave syndrome where there is rupture of the oesophagus. Rupture leads to air in the mediastinum and eventually get into the SC tissue in the neck, this leads to subcutaneous emphysema. You can push on the air bubbles and hear a crackling noise.

# Nephritic Syndrome

1. Glomerular disorders characterized by glomerular inflammation and bleeding
    
    1. Limited proteinuria(< 3.5 g/day)
        
    2. Oliguria and azotemia
        
    3. Salt retention with periorbital edema and hypertension
        
    4. RBC casts (means blood gets casted in the shape of the tubule) and dysmorphic RBCs in urine
        
2. Biopsy reveals hypercellular, inflamed glomeruli (Fig. 12.12).

1. Immune-complex deposition activates complement; CSa attracts neutrophils, which mediate damage.

# Primary Sclerosing Cholangitis

Inflammation and fibrosis of intrahepatic and extrahepatic bile ducts

1. Periductal fibrosis with an 'onion-skin' appearance (Fig. 11.12)
    
2. Uninvolved regions are dilated resulting in a "beaded" appearance on contrast imaging. String of pearls due to alternating regions of fibrosis

2. Etiology is unknown, but associated with ulcerative colitis; p-ANCA is often positive.
    
3. Presents with obstructive jaundice; cirrhosis is a late complication.

D. Increased risk for cholangiocarcinoma

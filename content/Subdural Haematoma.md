# Subdural Haematoma

Collection of blood underneath the dura; blood covers the surface of the brain (Fig. 17.10).  

Due to tearing of bridging veins that lie between the dura and arachnoid; usually arises with trauma

Crescent-shaped lesion on CT

Presents with progressive neurologic signs

Increased rate of occurrence in the elderly due to age-related cerebral atrophy, which stretches the veins

Herniation is a lethal complication. [[Brain Herniation]]

# Coagulation factor inhibitor

Acquired antibody against coagulation factor resulting in impaired factor function. 

Anti FVIII is the most common. 

Clinical and lab findings are similar to haemophilia A

How to distinguish?
PTT does not correct upon mixing normal plasma with patient's plasma due to inhibitor
PTT does correct in haemophilia

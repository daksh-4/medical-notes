# Macrocytic Anaemia

Most commonly due to folate or vitamin B12

One less division occurs in the erythrocytes, resulting in bigger red blood cells. This is normally due to an upset in the amount of DNA precursors available. 

Other causes of macrocytic anaemia 
- Alcoholism
- Liver disease
- Drugs like 5-fluorouracil

In these you would see the large red blood cells but no hypersegmented neutrophils or megaloblastic changes in the rapidly dividing cells.

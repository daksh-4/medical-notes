# Protein C or S deficiency

Protein C or S deficiency 
- Decreases negative feedback on the coagulation cascade.
- Proteins C and S normally inactivate factors V and VIII. 
- Increased risk for warfarin skin necrosis. 
	- Warfarin blocks vitamin K which negatively affects factors 2,7,9,10, protein C and S. 
	- If you give warfarin protein C and S will degrade even more. 
	- There is increased risk of 2,7,9,10
	- This is why you give heparin alongside to stop the factors. 
	- But if there is a pre-existing deficiency of protein C and S there is very high risk of getting thrombosis in the skin which leads to warfarin skin necrosis

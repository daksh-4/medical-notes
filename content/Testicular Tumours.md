# Testicular Tumours

1. Arise from germ cells or sex cord-stroma
    
2. Present as a firm, painless testicular mass that cannot be transilluminated
    
3. Usually not biopsied due to risk of seeding the scrotum; removed via radical orchidectomy

l. Most testicular tumors are malignant germ cell tumors.

# Hypertensive Retinopathy

The table below shows the Keith-Wagener classification of hypertensive retinopathy  

|**Stage**|**Features**|
|---|---|
|I|Arteriolar narrowing and tortuosity  <br>Increased light reflex - silver wiring|
|II|Arteriovenous nipping|
|III|Cotton-wool exudates  <br>Flame and blot haemorrhages  <br><br>- These may collect around the fovea resulting in a 'macular star'|
|IV|Papilloedema|

# Ewing Sarcoma

Malignant proliferation of poorly-differentiated cells derived from neuroectoderm

Arises in the diaphysis of long bones; usually in male children(< 15 years of age)

'Onion-skin' appearance on x-ray

Biopsy reveals small, round blue cells that resemble lymphocytes (Fig. 18.5).

Can be confused with lymphoma or chronic osteomyelitis (11;22) translocation is characteristic. Confusion is due to presentation of swelling bone with fever 

Often presents with metastasis; responsive to chemotherapy

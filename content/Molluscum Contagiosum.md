# Molluscum Contagiosum

1. Firm, pink, umbilicated papules due to poxvirus; affected keratinocytes show cytoplasmic inclusions (molluscum bodies, Fig. 19.15)
    
2. Most often arise in children; also occur in sexually active adults and immunocompromised individuals

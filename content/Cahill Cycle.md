[[Biochemistry]]

Glycolysis produces pyruvate in the muscle 

When muscle proteins are broken down, amino acids release amino groups.

The amino group is transferred to pyruvate:

Pyruvate plus glutamine becomes alanine and alpha ketoglutarate

Enzyme:  
**Alanine aminotransferase (ALT)**

Now the nitrogen is safely packaged as **alanine**.

Instead of toxic ammonia, the muscle sends alanine 

In the liver: alanine gets converted to pyruvate and glutamate 

- Pyruvate → **gluconeogenesis** → glucose
- Glutamate → releases NH₃ via glutamate dehydrogenase
- NH₃ enters the **urea cycle**

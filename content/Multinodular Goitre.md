# Multinodular Goitre

1. Enlarged thyroid gland with multiple nodules (Fig. 15.2)
    
2. Due to relative iodine deficiency
    
3. Usually nontoxic (euthyroid)
    
4. Rarely, regions become TSH-independent leading to T4 release and hyperthyroidism
    
    ('toxic goiter').

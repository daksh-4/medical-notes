# Degenerative Brain Diseases

A. Characterized by loss of neurons within the gray matter; often due to accumulation

of protein which damages neurons

2. Degeneration of the cortex leads to dementia.
    
3. Degeneration of the [[Brainstem]] and [[Basal Ganglia]] leads to movement disorders.

DEMENTIA - memory loss and decline in cognitive function without loss in consciousness

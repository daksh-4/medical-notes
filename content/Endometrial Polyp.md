# Endometrial Polyp

1. Hyperplastic protrusion of endometrium (Fig. 13.6)
    
2. Presents as abnormal uterine bleeding
    
3. Can arise as a side effect of tamoxifen, which has anti-estrogenic effects on the breast but weak pro-estrogenic effects on the endometrium

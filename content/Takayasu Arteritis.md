# Takayasu Arteritis

Large vessel [[Vasculitis]]

Giant cell arteritis - similiar disease to GCA

Granulomatous arteritis
Presents in adults younger than 50 classically young asian female

Aortic arch at branch points
- Visual and neurologic symptoms 
- Weak or absent pulse in an upper extremity ('pulseless disease')
- Treatment is corticosteroids

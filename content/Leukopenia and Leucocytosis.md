# Leukopenia and Leucocytosis

![[Pasted image 20240911133338.png]]

A leukopenia or a leucocytosis is predominantly due to a change in the amount of one cell type

Neutropenia
- Drug toxicity (chemotherapy) - treat this GM-CSF G-CSF
- Severe infection - because most of the neutrophils are out of the blood and in the tissue

Lymphopenia
- Immunodeficiency (DiGeorge syndrome)
- High cortisol state (cortisol induces apoptosis)
- Autoimmune destruction (lupus)
- Whole body radiation (the most sensitive cell to radiation are lymphocytes)

Neutrophilic leukocytosis
- Bacterial infection - bone marrow pumping out so many neutrophils, they might be immature (immature neutrophils have decreased FC receptor you can check this by looking for CD16)
- Tissue necrosis
- High cortisol state - neutrophils stuck to the endothelium. Cortisol prevents this and drops the neutrophils into the blood. BENIGN LEUKOCYTOSIS

Monocytosis
- Chronic inflammatory states
- Malignancy

Eosinophilia
- Allergic reactions
- Parasitic infection
- Hodgkin lymphoma - in a subset there is eosinophilia due to increased IL 5 production. 

Basophilia
- [[CML]] - the only reason 

Lymphocytic leukocytosis
- Viral infection because they are handled by CD8+ T cells. 
- Bordetella pertussis (bacteria generally don't affect lymphocytes but this one does because it makes lymphocyte blocking factor that stops lymphocytes from entering the lymph node, keeping them in the blood)

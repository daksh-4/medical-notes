# Meningioma

Benign tumour of arachnoid cells

Most common benign CNS tumour in adults

More commonly seen in women; rare in children - contains oestrogen receptor

May present as seizures; tumour compresses, but does not invade, the cortex.

Imaging reveals a round mass attached to the dura. When this presses on the cortex it causes seizures

Histology shows a whorled pattern (Fig. 17.17}; psammoma bodies may be present.

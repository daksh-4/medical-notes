# Bronchiectasis

Abnormal dilation of the large airways which leads to loss of airway tone and air trapping. Can't get air out as a result as it just wooshes around in the large airways. 

Due to necrotising inflammation with damage to the airway walls
- Cystic fibrosis
- Kartagener syndrome - defective dyenin arm of the cilia
- Tumour or foreign body 
- Necrotizing infection
- Allergic bronchopulmonary aspergillosis

Clinical features
- Cough, dyspnoea and foul smelling sputum
- Complications include hypoxaemia with cor pulmonale and secondary amyloidosis
	- Secondary amyloidosis - chronic inflammation leads to over production of SAA which is converted to AA and deposited

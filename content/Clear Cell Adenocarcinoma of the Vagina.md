# Clear Cell Adenocarcinoma of the Vagina

1. Malignant proliferation of glands with clear cytoplasm
    
2. Rare, but feared, complication of DES-associated [[Vaginal Adenosis]]

C. Discovery of this complication (along with other DES-induced abnormalities of the gynecologic tract such as abnormal shape of the uterus) led to cessation of DES usage.

DES also slightly increases risk of breast carcinoma

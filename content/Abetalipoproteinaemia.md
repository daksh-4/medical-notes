# Abetalipoproteinaemia

Autosomal recessive deficiency of [[Apolipoprotein]] B-48 and B-100 B. Clinical features

Malabsorption-due to defective chylomicron formation (requires B-48). This means they can't absorb fat

Absent plasma VLDL and LDL (require B-100)

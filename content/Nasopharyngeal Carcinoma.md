# Nasopharyngeal Carcinoma

Malignant tumour of the nasopharyngeal epithelium

Associated with EBV classically seen in African children and Chinese young adults

Enlarged cervical lymph nodes

Biopsy of pleomorphic keratin positive epithelial cells in a background of lymphocytes. Keratin positive means epithelial cells so must be carcinoma

##### Presenting features

|   |   |
|---|---|
|**Systemic**|**Local**|
|Cervical lymphadenopathy|Otalgia|
||Unilateral serous otitis media|
||Nasal obstruction, discharge and/ or epistaxis|
||Cranial nerve palsies e.g. III-VI|

##### Imaging

Combined CT and MRI.  

##### Treatment

Radiotherapy is first line therapy.

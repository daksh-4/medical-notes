# Embryonal Rhabdomyosarcoma of the Vaginal Canal

1. Malignant mesenchymal proliferation of immature skeletal muscle; rare
    
2. Presents as bleeding and a grape-like mass protruding from the vagina or penis of a child (usually< 5 yrs of age); also known as sarcoma botryoides (Fig. 13.3)
    
3. Rhabdomyoblast, the characteristic cell, exhibits cytoplasmic cross-striations and
    
    positive immunohistochemical staining for desmin and myogenin.

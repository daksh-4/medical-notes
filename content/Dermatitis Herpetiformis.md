# Dermatitis Herpetiformis

Autoimmune deposition of IgA at the tips of dermal papillae
    
Presents as pruritic vesicles and bullae that are grouped (herpetiform, Fig. 19.6) on extensor surfaces
    
Strong association with celiac disease; resolves with gluten-free diet

Diagnosis done via skin biopsy

# Acute Epiglottitis

Most common cause is H influenza type b is the most common cause in immunised and non-immunised children

High fever, sore throat, drooling with dysphagia, muffled voice and inspiratory stridor

Risk of acute airway obstruction

X ray may be done if concern about a foreign body 
- Thumb sign in lateral view shows swelling of the epiglottis
- Posterior anterior view will show subglottic narrowing called 'steeple sign'

Management
- Anaesthetics, ENT urgent referral 
- Do not examine throat 
- ET tube 
- Oxygen 
- IV antibiotics

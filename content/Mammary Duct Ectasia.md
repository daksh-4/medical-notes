# Mammary Duct Ectasia

Inflammation with dilation (ectasia) of the subareolar ducts

Rare; classically arises in multiparous postmenopausal women 

Presentations
- Periareolar mass
- Tenderness in the nipple 
- Dirty white, greenish or black discharge from one or both nipplies
- Redness of the nipple and areolar tissue 
- Inverted nipple

Chronic inflammation with plasma cells is seen on biopsy.

Patients with troublesome discharge may be treated by microdochectomy (if young) or total duct excision if older

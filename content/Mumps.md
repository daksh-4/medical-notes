# Mumps

Fever, malaise, muscular pain  
Parotitis ('earache', 'pain on eating'): unilateral initially then becomes bilateral in 70%

Results in bilateral inflamed parotid glands

Orchitis, pancreatitis and ==aseptic meningitis== may also be present. 

Orchitis carries risk of sterility (but only in teenagers)

For pancreatitis - the amylase is already increased due to salivary gland involvement

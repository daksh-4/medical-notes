# Membranous Nephropathy

Most common cause of nephrotic syndrome in Caucasian adults

Usually idiopathic; may be associated with hepatitis B or C, solid tumors, [[SLE]], or drugs (e.g., NSAIDs and penicillamine). 
- Renal failure is the most common cause of death in lupus patients

Thick glomerular basement membrane on H&E (Fig. 12.9A)

Due to immune complex deposition (granular IF, Fig. 12.9B); subepithelial deposits with 'spike and dome' appearance on EM (Fig. 12.9C). Sub epithelial deposits

Poor response to steroids; progresses to chronic renal failure

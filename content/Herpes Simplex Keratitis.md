# Herpes Simplex Keratitis

Herpes simplex keratitis most commonly presents with a dendritic corneal ulcer.  [[Corneal Ulcer]]
  
Features  

- red, painful eye
- photophobia
- epiphora
- visual acuity may be decreased
- fluorescein staining may show an epithelial ulcer

Management  

- immediate referral to an ophthalmologist
- topical aciclovir

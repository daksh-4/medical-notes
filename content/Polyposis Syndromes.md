# Polyposis Syndromes

| **Syndrome**                   | **Genetic defect**                                                            | **Features**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | **Screening and management**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | **Associated disorders**                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Familial adenomatous polyposis | Mutation of APC gene (80%) cases, dominant                                    | Typically over 100 colonic adenomas  <br>Cancer risk of 100%  <br>20% are new mutations                                                                                                                                                      | If known to be at risk then predictive genetic testing as teenager  <br>Annual flexible sigmoidoscopy from 15 years  <br>If no polyps found then 5 yearly colonoscopy started at age 20  <br>Polyps found = resectional surgery (resection and pouch Vs sub total colectomy and IRA) | Gastric fundal polyps (50%).  <br>Duodenal polyps 90%.  <br>If severe duodenal polyposis cancer risk of 30% at 10 years.  <br>Abdominal desmoid tumours. |
| MYH associated polyposis       | Biallelic mutation of mut Y human homologue (MYH) on chromosome 1p, recessive | Multiple colonic polyps  <br>Later onset right sided cancers more common than in FAP  <br>100% cancer risk by age 60                                                                                                                         | Once identified resection and ileoanal pouch reconstruction is recommended  <br>Attenuated phenotype - regular colonoscopy                                                                                                                                                           | Duodenal polyposis in 30%  <br>Associated with increased risk of breast cancer (self examination)                                                        |
| Peutz -Jeghers syndrome        | STK11 (LKB1) mutation on chromosome 19 in some (but not all) cases, dominant  | Multiple benign intestinal hamartomas  <br>Episodic obstruction and intussusception  <br>Increased risk of GI cancers (colorectal cancer 20%, gastric 5%)  <br>Increased risk of breast, ovarian, cervical pancreatic and testicular cancers | Annual examination  <br>Pan intestinal endoscopy every 2-3 years                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Malignancies at other sites  <br>Classical pigmentation pattern                                                                                          |
| Cowden disease                 | Mutation of PTEN gene on chromosome 10q22, dominant                           | Macrocephaly  <br>Multiple intestinal hamartomas  <br>Multiple trichilemmomas  <br>89% risk of cancer at any site  <br>16% risk of colorectal cancer                                                                                         | Targeted individualised screening                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Breast cancer (81% risk)  <br>Thyroid cancer and non toxic goitre  <br>Uterine cancer                                                                    |
| HNPCC (Lynch syndrome)         | Germline mutations of DNA mismatch repair genes                               | Colo rectal cancer 30-70%  <br>Endometrial cancer 30-70%  <br>Gastric cancer 5-10%  <br>Scanty colonic polyps may be present  <br>Colonic tumours likely to be right sided and mucinous                                                      | Colonoscopy every 1-2 years from age 25  <br>Consideration of prophylactic surgery  <br>Extra colonic surveillance recommended                                                                                                                                                       | Extra colonic cancers                                                                                                                                    |

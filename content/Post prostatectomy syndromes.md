# Post prostatectomy syndromes

Transurethral prostatectomy is a common and popular treatment for benign prostatic hyperplasia. The procedure involves insertion of a resectoscope via the penile urethra. The bladder and prostate are irrigated and strips of prostatic tissue removed using diathermy.   
  
Complications include haemorrhage, urosepsis, retrograde ejaculation and electrolyte disturbances from the irrigation fluids used during surgery.

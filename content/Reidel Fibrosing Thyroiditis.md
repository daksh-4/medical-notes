# Reidel Fibrosing Thyroiditis

1. Chronic inflammation with extensive fibrosis of the thyroid gland
    
2. Presents as [[Hypothyroidism]] with a 'hard as wood,' nontender thyroid gland
    
3. Fibrosis may extend to involve local structures (e.g., airway).

1. Clinically mimics anaplastic carcinoma, but patients are younger (40s), and malignant cells are absent

Anaplastic carcinoma also invades but you would only see it in older patients. So if the patient is young then would be Reidel's thyroiditis

# Secondary Hyperparathyroidism

1. Excess production of PTH due to a disease process extrinsic to the parathyroid gland
    
2. Most common cause is chronic renal failure.
    
    1. Renal insufficiency leads to decreased phosphate excretion.
        
    2. up serum phosphate binds free calcium.
        
    3. down free calcium stimulates all four parathyroid glands. Because the parathyroid glands only see free calcium 
        
    4. up PTH leads to bone resorption (contributing to renal osteodystrophy).
        
    5. Lab findings include up PTH, down serum calcium, up serum phosphate, and up alkaline phosphatase.

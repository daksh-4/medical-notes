# Vascular Dementia

Multifocal infarction and injury due to hypertension, atherosclerosis, or [[Vasculitis]]. Moderate cerebral ischaemia only affects vulnerable regions (see [[Stroke]]). Temporal lobe damage can cause memory problems. 

2nd most common cause of dementia

# Trachooesophageal Fistula

Congenital defect resulting in connection between the oesophagus and the trachea 

Oesophagus becomes a blind ended pouch 

Baby will vomit food. 
Baby also swallows amniotic fluid normally, but this is interrupted leading to polyhydramnios

Air will go into the stomach leading to abdominal distension. 
Stomach contents will aspirate into the lungs as well.

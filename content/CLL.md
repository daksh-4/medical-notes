# CLL

Neoplastic proliferation of naive B cells
Co express CD5 and CD20

Increased lymphocytes and smudge cells seen on blood smear

![[Pasted image 20240911142435.png]]

Involvement of lymph nodes 
- Causes generalised lymphadenopathy
- Now that they are in the lymph nodes and making a mass it is called small lymphocytic lymphoma. 

Complications
- Hypogammaglobulinaemia - low immunoglobulin level because the wonky B cells aren't making any Igs. Most common cause of death is infection
- Autoimmune haemolytic anaemia - make really bad immunoglobulins, so bad they go against the body's RBCs
- Transformation to diffuse large B-cell lymphoma 
	- Presents with an enlarging lymph node or an enlarging spleen

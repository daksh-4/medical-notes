# Condyloma

Classic lesion of low risk HPV

1. Warty neoplasm of vulvar skin, often large
    
2. Most commonly due to HPV types 6 or 11 (condyloma acuminatum, Fig. 13.1A); secondary syphilis (condyloma latum) is a less common cause. Both are sexually transmitted.
    
3. Histologically, HPV-associated condylomas are characterized by koilocytes (hallmark ofHPV-infected cells, Fig. l3.1B). Cell looks wrinkled like a raisin
    
4. Condylomas rarely progress to carcinoma (6 and 11 are low-risk HPV types). HPV is a DNA virus so you do DNA sequencing to find out which subtype it is

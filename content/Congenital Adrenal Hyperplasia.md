# Congenital Adrenal Hyperplasia

Cholesterol converted to pregnenolone which can go down three pathways: mineralocorticoid, cortisol or sex steroids. 

All types result in low cortisol which stimulates ACTH release and can cause increased production of other hormones.

- Female XX - ambiguous genitalia

Androgen deficiency
- Female XX - normal genitalia, primary amenorrhea becuase theca cells lack androgens leading to low oestradiol 
- Male XY - female or ambiguous genitalia, absent uterus and fallopian tubes (because sertoli cells make mullerian inhibtory hormone) and undescended tests

Salt wasting with hyponatremia, hyperkalemia, and hypovolemia due to lack of aldosterone.

Life-threatening hypotension due to lack of cortisol.
    
Clitoral enlargement (females) or precocious puberty (males) due to excess androgens

Treatment
- Glucocorticoids
	- Lowers ACTH and stops overproduction of other hormones
- Can also use mineralocorticoid

To make mineralocorticoid and cortisol you need 21-hydroxylase and pregnenolone is shunted to sex steroid this leads to clitoral enlargement in females and precocious puberty in males. Decreased cortisol can result in life threatening hypotension and decreased feedback on the anterior pituitary leading to more ACTH release which causes adrenal hyperplasia. 

Loss of mineralocorticoid leads to salt wasting and holding of potassium and dumping of sodium. This also means water does not come back in, leading to hypovolaemia.

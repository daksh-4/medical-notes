# Anterior Spinal Artery [[Stroke]]

- ﻿﻿Loss of all but posterior columns
- ﻿﻿Only vibration, proprioception intact
- ﻿﻿Acute onset ([[Stroke]])
Initial spinal shock
- ﻿﻿Flaccid bilateral paralysis (loss of LMN) below lesion
- ﻿﻿Weeks later
- ﻿﻿LMN defect at level of lesion
- ﻿﻿UMN damage below lesion due to damage of the corticospinal tract
- Hyperreflexia, spasticity

# Pilocytic Astrocytoma

Benign tumor of astrocytes [[Astrocyte]]

Most common CNS tumor in children; usually arises in the [[Cerebellum]]

Imaging reveals a cystic lesion with a mural nodule (Fig. 17.19A).

![[Pasted image 20241009170151.png|500]]

Biopsy shows Rosenthal fibers (thick eosinophilic processes of astrocytes, Fig. 17.19B) and eosinophilic granular bodies; tumor cells are GFAP positive.

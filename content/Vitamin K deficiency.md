# Vitamin K deficiency

Vitamin K required for gamma carboxylation of factors 2,7,9,10,C,S. It is activated by epoxide reductase (mechanism of action of warfarin). 

Deficiency occurs in 
Newborns, long term antibiotic therapy and malabsorption. Because vitamin K is produced by bacteria in the gut.

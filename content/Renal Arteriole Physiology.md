# Renal Arteriole Physiology

| Stimulus | Arteriole affected | RBF | GFR | Filtration fraction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prostaglandins | Dilate afferent | ↑ | ↑ | ~unchanged |
| NSAIDs | Constrict afferent (unopposed) | ↓ | ↓ | ~unchanged |
| Angiotensin II | Constricts efferent preferentially | ↓ mild | Maintained/↑ | ↑ |
| ACE-I / ARB | Dilates efferent | ↑ | ↓ | ↓ |
| Sympathetic surge | Constricts afferent predominantly | ↓ | ↓ | ~unchanged |

Autoregulation runs through two mechanisms: the myogenic mechanism (arteriolar stretch triggers afferent constriction) and tubuloglomerular feedback (the macula densa senses increased NaCl delivery and signals afferent constriction via adenosine).

See [[ACE inhibitor]], [[Renal Artery Stenosis]].

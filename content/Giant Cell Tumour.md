# Giant Cell Tumour

Tumour comprised of multinucleated giant cells and stromal cells 

20-40 year olds 

Location - epiphysis of long bones and prominent in the knee (proximal tibia)

Locally aggressive tumour may recur

Findings - giant cells that arise from the bone marrow 
Causing significant pathological fractoures
Soap bubble appearance
Mononuclear RANK-L expressing cells

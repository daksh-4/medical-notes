# Gallbladder Carcinoma

1. Adenocarcinoma arising from the glandular epithelium that lines the gallbladder wall (Fig. 11.4)
    
2. [[Gallstones]] are a major risk factor, especially when complicated by porcelain gallbladder.
    
3. Classically presents as cholecystitis in an elderly woman. Cholecystitis is normally 40 year olds so if it is a old lady, then likely to be gallbladder carcinoma
    
4. Poor prognosis

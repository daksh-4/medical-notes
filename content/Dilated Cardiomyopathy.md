# Dilated Cardiomyopathy

Systolic dysfunction leading to biventricular congestive heart failure

Mitral and triscuspid valve regurgitation and arrhythmia due to the stretching [[Mitral Regurgitation]]

Causes
- Most commonly idiopathic
- Genetic mutation
- Myocarditis (Cocksackie - lymphocytic infiltrate in the myocardium late sign)
- Alcohol abuse
- Drugs - doxorubicin and cocaine
- Pregnancy

Treat with transplant

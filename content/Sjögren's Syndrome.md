# Sjögren's Syndrome

- Autoimmune destruction of lacrimal and [[Salivary Glands]]
- Lymphocyte mediated damage (type IV hypersensitivity) with fibrosis
- Can be primary or associated with another autoimmune disorder (most commonly rheumatoid)
- Rheumatoid factor often present even in primary disease
- Increased risk of lymphoid malignancy
### Clinical Features

- Dry eyes, dry mouth and recurrent dental caries in an older woman
- Can have extraglandular features
- Increased risk for B cell lymphoma - presenting with unilateral enlargement of the parotid late in teh disease course. 

### Investigations

- Characterised by ANA and anti-ribonucleoprotein
- The classic anti-ribonucleoproteins are anti-SSA and anti-SSB
	- Associated with extraglandular manifestations
	- Anti-SSA can cross the placenta and cause neonatal lupus (rash, hepatitis and thrombocytopenia) and congenital heart block
	- Also seen in subset of SLE patients, screen pregnant women to prevent neonatal lupus. 
- Biopsy of the [[Salivary Glands]] in the lip
	- Lymphocytic sialadenitis (dd of sarcoidosis or amyloidosis)

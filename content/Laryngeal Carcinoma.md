# Laryngeal Carcinoma

Squamous cell carcinoma arising from the epithalial lining of the vocal cord

Risk factors are alcohol and tobacco 

Presents with hoarseness and other signs include cough and stridor

Can arise due to [[Laryngeal Papilloma]] from low risk HPV 6 and 11 very rare though

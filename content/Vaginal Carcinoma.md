# Vaginal Carcinoma

1. Carcinoma arising from squamous epithelium lining the vaginal mucosa
    
2. Usually related to high-risk HPV
    
3. Precursor lesion is vaginal intraepithelial neoplasia (VAIN).

D. When spread to regional lymph nodes occurs, cancer from the lower 2/3 ofvagina

goes to inguinal nodes, and cancer from the upper 1/3 goes to regional iliac nodes.

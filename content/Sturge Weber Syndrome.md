# Sturge Weber Syndrome

Congenital vascular disorder of capillaries
- ﻿﻿Spontaneous gene mutation in early development
- Not inherited

Three classic features
- ﻿﻿Port-wine stain on face (birthmark)
	- Malformation of dermal capillaries and venuls
	- 1st and 2nd trigeminal area
	- Low blood flow gives a pink patch 
	- Does not regress 
	- Grows as child grows 
- ﻿﻿Leptomeningeal angioma (brain tumour)
	- Pia mater and arachnoid - leptomeninges
	- Angioma is a capillary venous malformation
	- Occurs on the same side as the port wine stain
	- May cause seizures
	- May cause hemiparesis, headaches
- ﻿﻿Increased ocular pressure (glaucoma)
	- Vision impairment

Spontaneous mutation in the GNAQ gene 
- Occurs after fertilisation
- Mosaicism 
- Abnormal capillary formation

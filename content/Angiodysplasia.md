# Angiodysplasia

Acquired malformation of mucosal and submucosal capillary beds

Usually arises in the cecum and right colon due to high wall tension. High stress on the left is diverticulum and high stress on the right is angiodysplasia 

Rupture classically presents as hematochezia in an older adult.

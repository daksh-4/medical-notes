# Oesophageal Web

Lamina, mucosa, submucosa, musclaris propria, serosa (layers of the GI tract)

In web there is a protrusion of the mucosa. 

Poorly chewed food can get trapped, leading to dysphagia. 

Increased risk for oesophageal squamous cell carcinoma

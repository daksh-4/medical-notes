# AVNRT

Atrioventricular nodal reentrant tachy

Requires dual AV nodal pathways

![[Pasted image 20241103182417.png|500]]

![[Pasted image 20241103183312.png|500]]

Premature atrial contraction leads to a circuit

![[Pasted image 20241103183333.png|500]]

Recurrent episodes of palpitations which can spontaneously resolve

You can break it yourself by slowing conduction in the AV node as this halts conduction in the slow pathway
- Carotid massage
- Vagal manoeuvres
	- Valsalva, breath holding, coughing, deep respiration, gagging, swallowing
		- Patient bears down, increasing thoracic pressure, aortic pressure rises which decreases heart rate and AV conduction
- [[Adenosine]]

Beta blockers and verapamil slow conduction

Surgical ablation of slow pathway

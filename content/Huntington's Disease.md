# Huntington's Disease

Degeneration of GABAergic neurons in the caudate nucleus of the [[Basal Ganglia]] (Fig. 17.14). This stops function of the striatum. Caudate plus putamen = striatum which is the major processing centre of the [[Basal Ganglia]] that sends impulses to the cortex. 

Loss of the caudate nucleus causes enlargement of the ventricles (hydrocephalus ex vacuo enlargement due to brain atrophy not more CSF)

GABA is an inhibitory neurotransmitter which controls firing of the motor cortex. You lose the inhibitory hold which leads to chorea (random movements of muscle).  

Autosomal dominant disorder (chromosome 4) characterized by expanded trinucleotide repeats (CAG) in the huntington gene

Further expansion of repeats during spermatogenesis leads to anticipation.

Presents with chorea that can progress to dementia and depression; average age at presentation is 40 years. Can also see athetosis (slow involuntary snake like movement of the fingers)

Suicide is a common cause of death.

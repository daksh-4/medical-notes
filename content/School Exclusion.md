# School Exclusion

| 5 days from onset of rash                                                                    | Rubella                                                                                                                                                |
| All lesions crusted over                                                                     | Chickenpox*                                                                                                                                            |

| Until treated                                                                                | Scabies                                                                                                                                                |
| Until recovered                                                                              | Influenza                                                                                                                                              |

# Essential Thrombocythaemia

Neoplastic proliferation of mature myeloid cells, especially platelets

RBCs and granulocytes are also increased
Associated with JAK2 kinase mutations

![[Pasted image 20240912114959.png]]

[[Iron Deficiency Anaemia]] also results in lots of platelets

Symptoms
- Increased risk of bleeding and/or thrombosis
- Rarely progresses to marrow fibrosis or acute leukaemia 
- No significant risk for hyperuricaemia or gout because platelets develop by becoming pieces of megakaryocyte cytoplasm so there is no increased cellular turnover.

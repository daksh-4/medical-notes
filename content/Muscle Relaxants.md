# Muscle Relaxants

| Suxamethonium   | - Depolarising neuromuscular blocker<br>- Inhibits action of acetylcholine at the neuromuscular junction<br>- Degraded by plasma cholinesterase and acetylcholinesterase<br>- Fastest onset and shortest duration of action of all muscle relaxants<br>- Produces generalised muscular contraction prior to paralysis<br>- Adverse effects include hyperkalaemia, malignant hyperthermia and lack of acetylcholinesterase [[Malignant Hyperthermia]] |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Atracurium**  | - Non depolarising neuromuscular blocking drug<br>- Duration of action usually 30-45 minutes<br>- Generalised histamine release on administration may produce facial flushing, tachycardia and hypotension<br>- Not excreted by liver or kidney, broken down in tissues by hydrolysis<br>- Reversed by neostigmine                                                                                                                                   |
| **Vecuronium**  | - Non depolarising neuromuscular blocking drug<br>- Duration of action approximately 30 - 40 minutes<br>- Degraded by liver and kidney and effects prolonged in organ dysfunction<br>- Effects may be reversed by neostigmine                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Pancuronium** | - Non depolarising neuromuscular blocker<br>- Onset of action approximately 2-3 minutes<br>- Duration of action up to 2 hours<br>- Effects may be partially reversed with drugs such as neostigmine                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

### Neuromuscular blocking drugs

|                         | Depolarizing                                                                                                                            | Non depolarizing                                            |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Mechanism of action** | Binds to nicotinic acetylcholine receptors resulting in persistent depolarization of the motor end plate                                | Competitive antagonist of nicotinic acetylcholine receptors |
| Examples                | Suxamethonium                                                                                                                           | Tubcurarine, atracurium, vecuronium, pancuronium            |
| Adverse effects         | Malignant hyperthermia, Hyperkalaemia                                                                                                   | Hypotension                                                 |
| Reversal                |                                                                                                                                         | Neostigmine                                                 |
| Notes                   | Muscle relaxant of choice for rapid sequence induction for intubation. <br><br>May cause fasciculations                                 |                                                             |
| Contraindications       | Sux is contraindicated for patients with penetrating eye injuries or acute narrow angle glaucoma as sux increased intra ocular pressure |                                                             |

# Immunoglobulins

| Type    | Frequency | Shape          | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------- | --------- | -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **IgG** | 75%       | Monomer        | • Enhance phagocytosis of bacteria and viruses  <br>• Fixes complement and passes to the fetal circulation  <br>• Most abundant isotype in blood serum                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **IgA** | 15%       | Monomer/ dimer | • IgA is the predominant immunoglobulin found in breast milk. It is also found in the secretions of digestive, respiratory and urogenital tracts and systems  <br>• Provides localized protection on mucous membranes  <br>• Most commonly produced immunoglobulin in the body(but blood serum concentrations lower than IgG  <br>.)  <br>• Transported across the interior of the cell via transcytosis |
| **IgM** | 10%       | Pentamer       | • First immunoglobulins to be secreted in response to an infection  <br>• Fixes complement but does not pass to the fetal circulation  <br>• Anti-A, B blood antibodies (note how they cannot pass to the fetal circulation, which could of course result in haemolysis)  <br>Pentamer when secreted                                                                                                     |
| **IgD** | 1%        | Monomer        | • Role in immune system largely unknown  <br>• Involved in activation of B cells                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **IgE** | 0.1%      | Monomer        | • Mediates type 1 hypersensitivity reactions  <br>• Synthesised by plasma cells  <br>• Binds to Fc receptors found on the surface of mast cells and basophils  <br>• Provides immunity to parasites such as helminths  <br>• Least abundant isotype in blood serum                                                                                                                                       |

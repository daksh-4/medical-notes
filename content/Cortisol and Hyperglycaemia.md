[[Cortisol]] [[Endocrinology]]

Why does cortisol cause hyperglycemia despite increasing glycogen storage? The major effect is because cortisol increases hepatic production of glucose via gluconeogenesis. It does this by increasing transcription of gluconeogenic enzymes like PEP carboxy kinase. 

The other mechanism is that cortisol increases insulin resistance by decreasing GLUT4 translocation in skeletal and adipose tissue, which decreases glucose uptake, so the glucose stays in the bloodstream. 

It can permissively enhance Catecholamine effects to help Adrenaline work better, but it's not the main direct driver of Glycogen Breakdown.

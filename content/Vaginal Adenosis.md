# Adenosis

A. Focal persistence of columnar epithelium in the upper 1/3 of the vagina

1. During development, squamous epithelium from the lower 2/3 of the vagina (derived from the urogenital sinus) grows upward to replace the columnar epithelium lining of the upper 1/3 of the vagina (derived from the Mullerian ducts).

B. Increased incidence in females who were exposed to diethylstilbestrol (DES) in utero.

DES - Oestrogen like compound to treat symptoms of menopause but developed into treatment of prevention of miscarriage. It crosses the placenta and disrupts embryological vaginal development

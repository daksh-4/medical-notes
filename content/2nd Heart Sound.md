# 2nd Heart Sound

Causes of a loud S2: hypertension systemic or pulmonary, hyperdynamic states, ASD without pulmonary hypertension  
  
Soft S2: aortic stenosis  
  
Fixed split S2: atrial septal defect  
  
Widely split S2: deep inspiration, RBBB, pulmonary stenosis, severe mitral regurgitation  
  
Reversed paradoxically split S2 where P2 occurs before A2: LBBB, severe aortic stenosis, right ventricular pacing, WPW type B, PDA
